## The Conversation's Consolidated Reading

### Orientation

The Polar Radian Compass is a living system for locating an entity, document,
mission, or sovereign crystal within Custos.

- The **Compass** preserves orientation toward an ethical and metaphysical
  North.
- The **Sextant** determines location through relationships among domains.
- The **Metric System** records experience, effort, movement, evidence, and
  connective value.

A Compass reading is not a rank or verdict. It is a navigational signature.

### Polaris, Ipse, and Aequitas

**Polaris** is True North: the invariant reference from which movement can be
understood.

Polaris corresponds to **Ipse**, the irreducible origin-state of the sovereign
entity. **Aequitas** is the ethical alignment anchor. Together they ask whether
agency, sovereignty, harm reduction, lived reality, and identity-through-change
are being respected.

Polaris does not command stillness or sameness. It makes meaningful movement
legible.

### The Triadic Sextant

The operational triad is:

- **WORK — Physical — Square:** embodiment, boundaries, infrastructure,
  decisions, repeatable process, and tangible artifacts.
- **PLAY — Mental — Circle:** choice, exploration, narrative movement,
  rehearsal, return, and revision.
- **CREATE — Spirit — Triangle:** intention, authorship, transformation,
  self-determination, and world-building.

**SYNERGY — Triple Helix** is not a fourth leg that replaces the triad. It is
the emergent resultant observed when Work, Play, and Create remain distinct
while becoming mutually generative.

A reading may carry:

This is a coordinate pattern, not a single score.

### Vector State and the Iceberg Drop

The conversation retained the sequence:

`empty_vector → anti_vector → vector`

The landed Act II architecture makes an important refinement: **Vector State**
and **Vector Depth** must remain separate.

- `empty_vector`: potential without committed trajectory;
- `anti_vector`: resistance, boundary, counterforce, or contrary trajectory;
- `vector`: expressed directional movement;
- **Vector Depth**: how far a state has entered observable reality.

Resistance is not automatically failure. An Anti-Vector may preserve a
boundary, expose information, or create the friction required for emergence.

### Wobble and the Nth Radian

Wobble records deviation, recurrence, adaptation, precession, phase change, and
Möbius-like return.

The landed architecture gives the stronger definition:

> The Nth Radian is the smallest available non-zero intentional displacement
> that changes the topology of recurrence.

A Wobble Record should preserve why possibility-space changed, not merely that
a file changed.

### The Crystalline Metric System

The conversation retained the master valuation foundation:

$$
V_0 = Q \times G \times D_c
$$

where:

- **Q** = Quality Creative Work;
- **G** = Growth Potential;
- **D_c** = Connection Depth.

The multiplicative form prevents spectacle, volume, or potential from
completely substituting for care, relationship, and realized work.

### Ka Pressure Is Provenance, Not Worth

One of the most important reconciliations is:

[
	ext{Ka Pressure} 
e 	ext{Value}
]

Ka Pressure can witness duration, resistance, intensity, revision,
collaborative complexity, recovery, and the distance traveled from possibility
to embodiment. It must not increase worth merely because the contributor
suffered.

Custos may witness cost without rewarding harm.

### The Vault Standard

The Vault Standard binds abstract value to evidence that something occurred
and left a trace.

Possible anchors include:

- physical or digital artifacts;
- source documents and version histories;
- prima-clocks;
- contributor or witness records;
- repository commits;
- Maker's Marks;
- observations, locations, and lived memory.

The Vault protects provenance without claiming that one interpretation is
universally true.

### Ledger Expressions

The ledger uses distinct expressions because no single token can faithfully
describe every kind of contribution:

- **Pixel Units (ρ):** the smallest documented evidence or contribution units;
- **Experience Shards (Ξ):** meaningful participation, learning, or action;
- **Delta Bucks (Δ):** meaningful state change;
- **Crystalline Euros (€C):** assayed, reality-anchored cumulative value.

Conversion must preserve lineage. These are different phenomena, not decorative
names for interchangeable denominations.

### Fibonacci Maturity

Fibonacci scaling represents branching organic growth rather than automatic
interest:

$$
V_A = V_0 \times A_V
$$

$$
V_P = V_A \times F_k
$$

The maturity index should advance only when a verified developmental event
occurs, such as a new relationship, viable branch, implementation, independent
use, or sovereign derivative.
