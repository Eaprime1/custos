# Queue

Staging ground for what has crossed in from outside but hasn't yet found its place.

Nothing in `queue/` is finished. Items pass through on their way somewhere else —
the vault, the Seep Artois, or wherever their carrier points.

## Artesium Weir

`queue/artesium-weir/` — the filter between the raw artesian flow and chain of
custody. Raw PDF prints become custos items here. See
[`artesium-weir/README.md`](./artesium-weir/README.md) for the routine.
