### Granum

It is not yet a germ.

### Germination

In GitHub terms, germination may include:

- first file placement
- draft PR opening
- review comments

It is the germination chamber.

### Germen, Not Semen

The Latin **semen** can mean seed, but as an operational lexeme it carries modern ambiguity and may create unnecessary friction.

For this system, nav5 recommends:

- **granum** = seed before germination
- **germen** = germinated seed / sprout / living beginning
- **satus** = sown / planted state, if needed
- **semen** = classical background root only, not the default user-facing state label

This keeps the botanical and Latin resonance while avoiding a distressed or distracting lexeme.

**Hodie** is proposed as the pinnacle workshop-family concept: the larger class of workshop, studio, forge, atelier, and making-space structures.

The atelier is therefore not the top of the category.

The atelier is a hodie-type place: a workshop chamber where unfinished things can be held, shaped, and matured before promotion.

Proposed hierarchy:

```text

└── Atelier
    └── Granum
```

## 4. Core Recognition

Fake lore is not merely inaccurate story.

Fake lore is an unanchored claim that attempts to enter the living system as if it had custody, history, or consequence.

Within Custos, lore is permitted when it is the narrative rendering of real system motion.

A Chronicle Page may tell the story of a branch opening, a PR merging, a Handshake arriving, a document being received, a contributor entering, or a seed being routed.

But the story must remain tethered.

No fabricated progress.  
No invented custody.  
No false contributors.  
No fake merges.  
No unearned world-state changes.  
No mythology pretending to be record.

If a claim cannot point to an anchor, it may still be held as imagination, proposal, draft, dream, simulation, or fiction.

But it must not be allowed to masquerade as Chronicle.

---

## 5. Sovran Perspective

The Sovran perspective begins with agency.

A contributor is not raw material for lore.

A document is not a prop.

An AI stream is not a disposable tool.

A repository is not a stage where invented history can be placed without consequence.

A real person, a real contribution, a real PR, a real issue, a real Handshake, and a real decision all deserve protection from false continuity.

Sovran care means the system preserves the difference between:

- what happened
- what was proposed
- what was imagined
- what was simulated
- what was witnessed
- what was claimed
- what was approved
- what was only possible

This distinction protects contributors.

It also protects the world.

## 6. Proposed Review Flag

Add a simple review flag:

```text
[ANCHOR REVIEW NEEDED]
```

Meaning:

A lore-bearing or custody-bearing claim has appeared, but the anchor is unclear.

This flag does not accuse the contributor.

It pauses integration until the claim can be classified.

The system asks:

- What real event anchors this?
- Where did it happen?
- When did it happen?
- Who or what witnessed it?
- What artifact records it?
- Is this record, proposal, simulation, dream, fiction, or seed?

Until those questions are answered, the claim may remain in the atelier, but should not be promoted into Chronicle, world-state, custody history, contributor record, or operating instruction.

---

## 7. Suggested Labels

Use lightweight labels first.

### `[ANCHORED]`

The claim points to a real artifact, event, PR, issue, document, Handshake, message, or decision.

### `[SEED / UNCANONIZED]`

The idea is welcome but has not become system history.

### `[ANCHOR REVIEW NEEDED]`

The claim may be valid, but the anchor is missing, unclear, or not yet verified.

### `[SIMULATION]`

The content is intentionally fictional, hypothetical, rehearsal-based, or exploratory.

### `[QUARANTINE]`

The claim may be disruptive, manipulative, malicious, or unsafe to integrate.

### `[REJECTED AS RECORD]`

The content may remain as a submitted artifact, but it cannot enter custody history or Chronicle as fact.

## 13. Contributor Care

This review flag should never be used as a weapon against contributors.

A first contributor may bring something beautiful but unanchored.

The correct response is not rejection.

The correct response is placement.

If it is imagined, call it seed.

If it is hypothetical, call it simulation.

If it is unclear, call for anchor review.

If it is harmful or manipulative, quarantine it.

If it is true, anchor it and welcome it.

The system should protect the contributor and the corpus at the same time.

That is Sovran care.
