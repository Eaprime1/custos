## Entry requirements

Begin with a short resident record: ID, form, source, origin, disposition, mission or null, and automation state. Add detail as the specimen needs it, not as a mandatory sixteen-field form. Keep task assignee separate from the lexeme's mission.

A developed note can add lemma and grammar, a marked first-person introduction, Eric's intention, navigo interpretation, historical evidence, project meaning, decision provenance, examples, uncertainty and revision history. Distinguish attested forms, reconstructed ancestry, concept history, project interpretation, serious play and implemented behavior.

Start research with a relevant Wikipedia page and link, identifying whether it covers the exact word or a related concept. Supplement it with lexical evidence. If no suitable page is found, record that gap rather than force a match. Do not invent an earlier origin or a verification result.
