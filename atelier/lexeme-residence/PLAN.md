## Entry requirements

Keep resident identity, adoption, development task, mission and automation state separate. A task assignee identifies who does work; an unassigned lexeme has no mission, regardless of who maintains its note.

A developed entry contains: stable ID; form and lemma; language; origin; first-person introduction; Eric's intent; navigo interpretation; sourced history; project meaning; decision provenance; examples and nonexamples; uncertainty; mission or null; implementation state; safety boundaries; revision history.

Distinguish attested forms, reconstructed ancestry, concept history, project interpretation, serious-play narration and implemented behavior. A related Wikipedia page is not proof of the Latin form. Do not invent an earlier origin or a verification result.
