## The what

In the Shepherd's words: Matenadaran would make a good character system peer to "valthram" and
"complexity", one of the characters and concepts; one of the evaluation, valuation and quantification
specialists. *What does the real history speak? Preservation seems to be a key aspect, part of the decision
process for how things are preserved within our system.*

## "valthram", now found (export dated 202610172338 UTC)

The earlier note said "valthram" appeared nowhere in this repo. The Shepherd then supplied a Gemini export
that describes it as the **Valthraman** (also written Velthraman) framework. The export is filed whole in
the Weir, with a carrier, at
`queue/artesium-weir/valthraman-gemini-export-202610172338.original.md` and
`queue/artesium-weir/valthraman-gemini-export.md`.

Two things to hold plainly:

- The export is a summary by an assistant of a Drive file named "Valthraman Synergy Json.pdf". That file
  has not been seen here. What the export says Valthraman is, is therefore second-hand, and this seed does
  not restate its claims as fact.
- The Shepherd's "complexity" may point at the export's line "All Complexity = 1". That is a reading, not
  confirmed. Say so if it is wrong.

## What the history gives the idea (from section 9, short)

The strongest thread for *preservation* is the colophon: a note inside a manuscript that names who held it
and who paid to free it. The object carried its own custody record. Sources disagree on the date of the
collection's founding and its size (about 17,000 to over 100,000), and that disagreement is itself useful: a
specialist in preservation can be plain about what cannot be counted.
