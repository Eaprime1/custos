## On Alpha-Omega Superposition

*New framework expansion — filed here at the Shepherd's direction, 202610142006.*

Traditional masculine/feminine role dynamics typically reduce to an alpha/beta
relationship — a hierarchy with a fixed dominant and a fixed subordinate. The project
proposes replacing this with **alpha and omega in superposition**.

If one party is alpha, the other is omega. If one is the stick, the other is the stone.
The roles are not fixed — they exist in superposition until observation collapses them
for a given interaction. Between alpha and omega lies the full spectrum where everything
that needs to fit will fit.

This is not a compromise or a blending. It is a structural replacement: hierarchy gives
way to complementarity in superposition. The stick and the stone are not ranked — they
are different forms of the same force, and which is which depends on the moment.

Framework mapping:
- Alpha ↔ Omega maps to Vector ↔ Anti-Vector
- The superposition state (before collapse) maps to Prime
- The spectrum between maps to the navigation space where Journey/Bestill/Justbe operate
- The stick/stone duality mirrors the `now_here ↔ nowhere` polarity already established
  in `.shadow-well/`
