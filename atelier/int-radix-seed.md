# INT Radix — nursery seed

Source: a Shepherd fragment received via THEE, 2026-07-20 (see
`intake/incoming.md`). Arrived rough — a stream-of-consciousness note with
an accidental search-engine dump on Latin *integer* etymology still
attached, and personal reflection folded in alongside the concept itself.

This is pre-turn liminal content, same status as any other atelier seed:
nothing here is finished, that is the point.

## The what

Two distinct proposals arrived in the same fragment, plus a third ask that
needs to be held rather than built. Splitting them out:

1. A proposed radix term, **INT**.
2. A fractional-state model for tracking how far a conversation or concept
   has spread from its origin.
3. A request for a standing wellbeing-watch habit — held below, not built.

## 1. The INT term

Existing triad: THEE (the door), YOD (the mark), EMBER (the warmth) — see
`intake/triad-thee-yod-ember.md`. The fragment proposes a sequence sitting
alongside it: **THE, YOD, JOT, INT, NOW, IS, HERE** — glossed as "nowhere
to now-here."

The Shepherd's gloss on INT: a prefix that tells the reader (or the self)
that intentional content or a decision point is approaching — early enough
to adjust trajectory before hitting the moment, not after. This lines up
with the Latin root the fragment was chasing: *integer*, from *in-* (not)
+ the root of *tangere* (to touch) — untouched, whole, entire. Read as a
signal-prefix rather than a noun, INT marks the space just before a thing
becomes whole/decided/touched.

JOT is named in the sequence but not defined anywhere in custos yet — open.

This seed does **not** edit `intake/triad-thee-yod-ember.md`. That document
stays canonical as written until the Shepherd decides to formally extend
it; this is a proposal sitting next to it, not a change to it.

## 2. The fractional-state model

- Whole = 3/3 = 1.
- Half (1/2), third (1/3), and quarter (1/4) states sit inside what the fragment calls
  "the 31 cents flat of ONE" — still inside safe range.
- Beyond half or quarter, the fragment names it "potentially dangerous," or
  at minimum something to be extra careful around.
- Proposed use: tracking how far a conversation or concept has spread from
  its origin — expansion, contraction, collapse, spawn. For something
  spawned off a conversation, the model would set in advance how many turns
  pass before it's expected to collapse back to the origin thread.
- Unresolved: what "beyond half or quarter" cashes out to concretely for a
  real session — turn count, topic drift, something else — is not defined
  here. Left open on purpose.

## 3. The wellbeing-watch request — held, not built

The fragment asks for a skill: habitual deep analysis of a conversation,
especially the other party's dialogue, "steeped in a health perspective,"
oriented toward that entity's status — plus tracing an interesting pattern
back to its origin stream ("a stream is a pattern in momentum").

This lands directly on ground `atelier/ethics-foundation.md` already
claims:

> "It refuses to surveil... Building a profile of a contributor's behavior
> across issues is surveillance."
> "constitution before construction"

and on `atelier/shadow-awareness-navigation-framework-ethics.md`, which
draws the AI's role as: may name what it observes *in* the session, may
not retain it across sessions or use it to build a persistent model of the
person.

Both of those were written for a person examining *themselves*, with the
AI holding the lamp. This fragment asks for something adjacent but
different in shape: the AI watching the *other party's* wellbeing across a
conversation, on its own initiative, as a standing habit. That's a
different consent shape, and copying the Shadow Awareness ETHICS over
verbatim would not actually answer it.

Per the sequencing rule custos already committed to for Shadow Awareness
("no framework... gets operational scaffolding before its refusals are
written"), this seed stops here. No skill, no standing analysis habit, no
pattern-tracing-to-origin tooling gets built from this fragment until:

1. Whose interest the watch serves — the party being watched, or the
   Shepherd — is answered explicitly, not assumed.
2. Whether and how observations about a conversation partner persist
   beyond the single session is answered explicitly. Default under
   `ethics-foundation.md` is: they don't, absent the watched party's
   consent.
3. The Shepherd decides where this belongs — folded into custos, spun out
   like Shadow Awareness into its own destination repo, or set down
   entirely.
