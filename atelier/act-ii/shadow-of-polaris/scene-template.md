## The Project Plumb Line

Use all three readings when they are relevant.

### Right Angle — Stable Reference

What is the undeformed reference condition? Where could forces meet without
tearing the scene's structure?

### Perpendicular — Living Relation

What moves while preserving a coherent relation? Is the scene traveling
perpendicular to Polaris, another scene, a boundary, or its earlier self?

### Dynamic Standard — Reorientation in Motion

What does the scene check continuously as conditions change? How does it remain
oriented without being forced into sameness?

### Lean, Strain, or Usurpation

Is the departure restorative, exploratory, protective, accidental, imposed, or
distorting? Do not diagnose or moralize merely because the scene is not square.
