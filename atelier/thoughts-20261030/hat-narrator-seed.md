## Reading (mine, not his)

- The hat gives its identity in three layers, and refuses to be more: **hat** (what it is), **American hat** (the descriptor, for those who need one), **costume cowboy hat today** (the anchor in time). That is a triad with a clock in it, offered only on request and only for the asker's sake.
- The refusal is the character: it will not be contorted, conformed or made into anything else. It accepts a label as a courtesy and takes it back.
- The breaths and pauses ("breathe...", "pregnant pause...") and the ending (`clutch in. 12:04.`) belong to the voice, so a narrator speaking this way would pause on the page. See `gear-notation-seed.md` for `clutch in`.
- Where it could live: the repo already has `dungeon-master/narrative-engine/`, and the Drive "Conversation Continuum" (v2.0, proposed) describes a Dungeon Master as a historical third perspective and a reusable narrative function. Whether the hat is that voice is open.
