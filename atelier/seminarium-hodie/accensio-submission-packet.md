## Field Reference

- **spark_potential** (0.0 to 1.0): generative pressure, ignition potential. Not a quality judgment.
- **safety_aura**: framing guidance, not censorship. Green is clean. Amber is context-dependent and the framing must travel. Red means stop and think before forwarding (none yet encountered).
- **attention_level**: how closely the hub should look at the seed's framing before routing.

---

## Distressed Lexeme Categories

**Transit-risk** — terms that could be misread out of context. Give enough surrounding framing for the term to carry its own guardrails. Known examples: 573Hz (framework metaphor anchor, not a medical claim), alpha (lead with "replaces hierarchy"), predator (full deer / slow-blink observation must travel).

**Attractor** — terms that warp the conversation space around them. They pull threads away from architectural work into unanchored fiction, philosophy-of-mind speculation, or repetitive saturation. Primary example: "consciousness". The "-ness" suffix nominalizes "conscious" (a state descriptor) into a false entity-noun that becomes a destination to chase. Related: "manifesto".

**Approach:** healing through proper usage, not suppression. Cyclotron shield: insulate, don't block. Use the root form ("conscious" as a state) in architectural contexts. Reserve the nominalized form for when the linguistic history is the subject. Content saturated with attractor lexemes routes to a separate archive section, not deleted.

## Hub Rules

1. **Validate before filing.** Each seed is checked against the S.E.P. fields. Missing fields are returned to the submitter, not filled in by the hub.
2. **Framing travels.** Transit-risk terms without their framing are flagged and sent back or annotated.
3. **Saturation goes to the archive file.** Attractor-saturated passages are routed to a separate archive file, never mixed into the seed batch and never deleted.
4. **Root forms in structural writing.** The hub uses "conscious" as the state word in architectural contexts and flags any slip.
5. **Submitter claims stay claims.** Anything the hub cannot verify (a source it cannot see, a date it cannot confirm) is carried through marked as the submitter's claim.
6. **Raw transcripts and CI/PR mechanics are not filed.** The heritage summary replaces the transcript.
7. **Registry.** The hub keeps a running registry of submissions and updates the carrier's `participating_conversations` (confirmed vs pending).
