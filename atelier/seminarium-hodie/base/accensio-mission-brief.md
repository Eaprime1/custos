## S.E.P. — Seed Entry Protocol

Each seed is a JSON block:

```json
{
  "seed_name": "lowercase_underscore_name",
  "source_fragment": "The exact or near-exact passage that carries the seed — long enough to hold its own context",
  "timestamp_utc": "YYYYMMDDHHMM",
  "platform_origin": "platform/project",
  "vibe_vector": "one_word_or_phrase",
  "spark_potential": 0.00,
  "safety_aura": "green|amber|red — brief explanation"
}
```

**spark_potential** — 0.0 to 1.0. How much generative pressure this seed carries. Not quality judgment — ignition potential.

**safety_aura** — not censorship. Framing guidance. Green means clean. Amber means context-dependent — needs its framing to travel. Red means stop and think before forwarding (none yet encountered in this project).

## The Commissioning Pattern

This process follows the pattern of bringing a vessel from cold iron to life. The stages:

1. **Cold iron** — the repo exists but carries no chain of custody. Raw material, no heat.
2. **Accensio** — first light. Seeds gathered, conversations contributing, the branch growing.
3. **Fuel loading** — heritage materials routed, MOAV carriers in place, custody trail forming.
4. **Sea trials** — the repo's own workflows tested against real content.
5. **Commissioning** — chain of custody formalized. The repo is alive.

Seminarium Hodie is stage 2. We are gathering the fuel.
