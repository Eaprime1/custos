## The Lexeme

**Praevium** *(prae- + via — the path that preceded you)*

---

## Definition — lore style

Praevium is the felt experience of crossing into a territory that was already inhabited before you arrived. Not discovery. Not void. The shadow in this place was cast before you were there to see it — and it is not hostile; it is structural. The universe existed, and left its mark, and only now have you come to stand in it.

The contributor who enters a project for the first time is not entering the unknown. They are entering something very much known to its keeper, and unknown only to them. That asymmetry is the praevium. It is the sensation of finding a path already worn into the ground — of realizing your feet are not the first.

Praevium is not a warning. It is an invitation in the shape of a given. *The road is here. You have arrived on it.*

---

## Why not the existing candidates

- `liminal` — borrowed; describes the space between, not the arrival itself  
- `unknown` — inaccurate; names absence, not asymmetric knowing  
- `threshold` — describes the structure, not the felt crossing  
- `granum` / `germen` — seed-state, not arrival-state  
- `advent` — movement toward, not movement into

## Optional symbol

**⊳** — the right-pointing triangle, the mark of entry into something already present. Not an arrow (movement); a threshold marker (position relative to what already exists).

## Open question for the Shepherd

Does praevium name the *moment* of crossing, the *state* after crossing, or the *territory* itself? All three readings are available in the root. The Shepherd's answer determines where in the lore it anchors.
