A well is not a folder yet. It is a **behavior**: what it does to what falls in. Ten
have been imagined. Following the Shepherd's "start with three, let it grow",
**three make up the Terminus** and seven wait. Take, bend or drop any of them.

---

## The Three

One gathers, one pours, one lets results emerge. The order is the Shepherd's
question ("would the order fit better as dark, salt pan, lumen?"), and the first
draft ran the other way, so both can be tried.

```
   dark spectrum ──▶ salt pan ──▶ lumen spectrum
     gather            pour          emerge
```

### 1. The Dark  *(the dark spectrum)*
`role: gather — things come together as a submission`
Black and still. Nothing that falls in echoes. It works like the cap on the end of a
line: it takes what arrives and does not send it back as noise. The dark is not
empty. It is where things are gathered before they have a shape, and a submission is
what they become when they are brought together.
- **Physics:** absorption, then gathering. Nothing here is turned away or lit.
- **Passes on when:** the gathered things hold together as one submission.
- **Outflow:** poured into the Salt Pan.
- **Holds back:** anything withdrawn keeps one line of residue, so a set-aside thing
  still teaches something (see the residue table in `trial-0.md`).
- **Kin:** the Shadow Well (`.shadow-well/`) is *before naming*; the Dark is where
  named things gather to be submitted.

### 2. The Salt Pan  *(the pour, and the pinnacle)*
`role: pour — the submission is poured in and what remains is the thing`
A flat where water has gone. What is left is white crystal. This is FOAH, the
crystallization matrix (Father of All Halides), as ground you can stand on. It is
**not a pool**. Here is where a document can **stop for changes** while it is in the
station's motion.
- **Physics:** evaporation. Water leaves; the thing stays.
- **Passes on when:** it can be re-wetted and survive. That is what pinnacle means here.
- **Outflow:** the project content in the repo, and `vault/spade-of-aces/` for the
  pinnacle.
- **Setting:** open sky, no roof: the middle of the line, under Polaris.

### 3. The Lumen  *(the lumen spectrum, starlight on a crystal)*
`role: emerge — results come out as light`
Starlight falls through an opening in a cave roof onto a crystal. A drop gathers on
the stalactite above, carries a little of the light down, and lands on the
stalagmite below, then in the pool. Each drop leaves a little mineral. Lumen is the
warm-without-heat light of Lacus Hope (`world/five-lakes.md`): not white, which is
too much, and not dark, which is too little.
- **Physics:** accretion by light. A stalagmite grows only by many small visits, and
  where stalactite and stalagmite meet they form a column.
- **What emerges:** results. What shape they take is undecided.
- **Setting:** the well nearest Polaris. Its light is the only light in the cave.
- **Why it comes last:** the Shepherd's order lets results *emerge* from the light,
  after the pour, and not gather ahead of it.

## Seven for Later

Named so they are not lost. None is scheduled.

### The Clear River Pool  *(Shepherd's image)*
`category: returns — finished work coming back from a stream or a navigo`
A shallow pool on a river, clear to the bottom. What arrives is already named and
drops over a small fall. **Passes on when:** it is legible without the author present.

### The Cauldron  *(Shepherd's image)*
`category: synthesis — several things that must become one`
Carried to, not arriving on its own. Heat is the Shepherd's attention, so an
unattended cauldron goes cold. **Passes on when:** the output is smaller than the
sum of its inputs. Slow cousin: the vinegar mother of **MOAV**.

### The Lexicon Cistern  *(Shepherd's "wiki with a search list")*
`category: terms — lexemes, definitions, the words the project coins`
A stone cistern you do not scoop from; you **lower a line and ask**, with a search
list at the rim. **Passes on when:** a term has a citable source. Reading never
lowers the level.

### The Echo Well
`category: questions — requests for another navigo's view`
A deep shaft. You drop a question and count; the **depth** is how long an answer
takes. An answer that returns is written on the wall, in place.

### The Geyser
`category: recurring — things that surface on a cadence`
A vent that erupts on its own interval. The eruption *is* the passing.

### The Glacier Tarn
`category: archive — kept, not finished`
Cold, dark blue, slow. Things frozen in it thaw under pressure and are not dead.
Thawing needs a named reason.

### The Thermal Spring
`category: urgent — needs attention now`
Hot, steaming, unmissable, and unable to hold anything long: a thing here cools
(gets tended) or boils off (goes stale).

## Design Rules for a New Well

1. **One category, one physics.** If a well needs two rules, it is two wells.
2. **The water makes the test.** People hold the gate, not the ordinary flow.
3. **Every well names where its results go.**
4. **A well teaches its category.** If the physics do not say what the category is,
   the well is decoration.
5. **Do not copy.** If the picture came from a story, keep the mechanic and change the water.
