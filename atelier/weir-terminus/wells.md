# The Wells

`suit: ♦️ Diamond — idea-gathering, nothing built`
`prima-clock: 202609300539`
`revised: 202609300711`

A well is not a folder yet. It is a **behavior**: what it does to what falls in. Ten
have been imagined, one per category of thing that arrives. Following the Shepherd's
"start with three, let it grow", **three are proposed to found the Terminus** and
seven wait. Take, bend or drop any of them.

---

## The Founding Three

One gathers, one ends, one returns. Together they are the smallest loop that closes.

```
   starlight ─▶ [ 1 Stalagmite Pool ] ─ crest ─▶ [ 2 Salt Pan ]   (what stays)
                       │
                       └─ spill ─▶ [ 3 Dark Pool ] ─ residue ─▶ back upstream
```

### 1. The Stalagmite Pool  *(the starlight crystal)*
`category: seeds — ideas that need more than one visit`
Starlight falls through an opening in a cave roof onto a crystal. A drop gathers on
the stalactite above, carries a little of the light down, and lands on the
stalagmite below, then in the pool. Each drop leaves a little mineral, so the
stalagmite grows only by many small visits.
- **Physics:** accretion. Glow is the count of **distinct keys** that have touched a
  seed, not the count of touches. One navigo tapping ten times is one glow.
- **Crest:** stalactite and stalagmite meeting is a **column**. A seed with glow from
  **three or more keys** has grown into a column and rises out of the pool.
- **Outflow:** `atelier/` as a concept, a mission candidate in `docs/mission-ideas.md`,
  or on to the Salt Pan.
- **Kin:** `queue/seed-weir/`, which keeps rows but cannot show *who else cared*.
- **Setting:** the well nearest Polaris. Its light is the only light in the cave.

### 2. The Salt Pan  *(the pinnacle)*
`category: pinnacle — the ♠️ that remains`
A flat where the water has gone. What is left is white crystal. This is FOAH, the
crystallization matrix (Father of All Halides), as ground you can stand on. It is
**not a pool**, which is the point.
- **Physics:** evaporation. Water leaves; the thing stays.
- **Crest:** a thing that cannot be dissolved back into water. Surviving being
  re-wetted *is* pinnacle status.
- **Outflow:** `vault/spade-of-aces/`.
- **Setting:** open sky, no roof: the far end of the line, under Polaris.

### 3. The Dark Pool  *(the terminator)*
`category: spill — what is finished with, set aside, or composted`
Black and still. Nothing that falls in echoes. It is the cap on the end of the line:
it takes what arrives and does not send it back as noise. Under the dark sits
**lumen**, the warm-without-heat light of Lacus Hope, so whatever is left slowly
turns to nutrient.
- **Physics:** recirculation. Dark water rises again upstream as fresh head.
- **Crest:** none; it takes all spill. It returns a **residue**: one line of what the
  thing taught before it went dark.
- **Two faces:** *void* (truly finished) and *lumen* (glowed once, may again).
- **Kin:** `COMPOSTED` in the seed weir; the near-side and far-side pull of Lacus Hope.
- **Polarity:** the Shadow Well is *before naming*; the Dark Pool is *after use*.
- **Not a maw:** it does not transform what falls in. A maw would make something
  new from it. The Dark Pool returns the same water, with a lesson.

## Seven for Later

Named so they are not lost. None is scheduled.

### The Clear River Pool  *(Shepherd's image)*
`category: returns — finished work coming back from a stream or a navigo`
A shallow pool on a river, clear to the bottom. What arrives is already named and
drops over the crest as a small fall. **Crest:** legible without the author present.
**Outflow:** `returns/`, `vault/`, or a mission.

### The Cauldron  *(Shepherd's image)*
`category: synthesis — several things that must become one`
Carried to, not arriving on its own. Heat is the Shepherd's attention, so an
unattended cauldron goes cold. **Crest:** the output is smaller than the sum of its
inputs. **Note:** this is close to a maw, since it makes one thing from several. It
stays here only while its output is a *convergence* (a summary that keeps the
originals intact). If it starts changing them, it belongs to Maw. Slow cousin: the
vinegar mother of **MOAV**.

### The Lexicon Cistern  *(Shepherd's "wiki with a search list")*
`category: terms — lexemes, definitions, the words the project coins`
A stone cistern you do not scoop from; you **lower a line and ask**, with a search
list at the rim. **Crest:** a term enters only with a citable source (the `lexeme`
issue's rule, made into terrain). **Outflow:** none; reading never lowers the level.

### The Echo Well
`category: questions — requests for another navigo's view`
A deep shaft. You drop a question and count; the **depth** is how long an answer
takes. An answer that returns is written on the wall, in place. Kin: `petitio`, but
with no seat and no sluice.

### The Geyser
`category: recurring — things that surface on a cadence`
A vent that erupts on its own interval. **Crest:** the eruption *is* the crest.
Kin: the `loop` skill and the hourly PR check-in.

### The Glacier Tarn
`category: archive — kept, not finished`
Cold, dark blue, slow. Things frozen in it thaw under pressure and are not dead:
`unoiam-lifecycle` Phase 4 given a body. **Crest:** thaw needs a named reason.

### The Thermal Spring
`category: urgent — needs attention now`
Hot, steaming, unmissable, and unable to hold anything long: a thing here cools
(gets tended) or boils off (goes stale). Anything still hot after a full turn is
escalated.

## Design Rules for a New Well

1. **One category, one physics.** If a well needs two rules, it is two wells.
2. **The crest is a test the water makes.** People hold the sluice, not the crest.
3. **Spill goes somewhere.** Every well names its overflow; the Dark Pool is the default.
4. **A well teaches its category.** If the physics do not say what the category is,
   the well is decoration.
5. **A weir does not transform.** If it changes what arrives, it is a maw.
6. **Do not copy.** If the picture came from a story, keep the mechanic and change the water.
