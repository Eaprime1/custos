# The Wells

`suit: ♦️ Diamond — idea-gathering, nothing built`
`prima-clock: 202609300539`

Ten candidate wells, one per category of thing that arrives at the Terminus. Each
has its own physics so the well itself says what its category is. The first
three are the Shepherd's images; the rest grew from them. Take, bend, or drop any.

A well is not a folder yet. It is a **behavior**: what it does to what falls in.

---

## The Catalog

### 1. The Clear River Pool  *(Shepherd's image)*
`category: returns — finished work coming back from a stream or a navigo`
A shallow pool on a river, clear to the bottom. Nothing hides. What arrives is
already named and simply passes through, dropping over the crest as a small fall.
- **Physics:** through-flow. A return sits at most one turn.
- **Crest:** it is legible without the author present.
- **Outflow:** `returns/`, `vault/`, or a mission.
- **Feeds from:** the seven streams in `returns/`.

### 2. The Stalagmite Pool  *(Shepherd's image)*
`category: seeds — ideas that need more than one visit`
Water runs down glowing stalagmites into a basin. Each drop leaves a little
mineral, so a stalagmite grows only by many small visits. It glows where it has
been touched.
- **Physics:** accretion. Glow is the count of **distinct keys** that have touched a
  thing, not the count of touches. One navigo tapping ten times is one glow.
- **Crest:** a stalagmite that reaches a set height (glow from three or more keys)
  rises out of the pool.
- **Outflow:** `atelier/` as a concept, or a mission candidate in `docs/mission-ideas.md`.
- **Kin:** `queue/seed-weir/`, which keeps rows but has no way to show *who else
  cared*.

### 3. The Cauldron  *(Shepherd's image)*
`category: synthesis — several things that must become one`
Not water that arrives on its own: things carried to it and cooked. Heat is the
Shepherd's attention, so an unattended cauldron goes cold.
- **Physics:** combination under heat. Two or more inputs go in, one input comes out,
  and the recipe is written down.
- **Crest:** the output must be smaller than the sum of the inputs.
- **Outflow:** a single carrier, e.g. the convergence notes between navigo.
- **Slow cousin:** the vinegar mother of **MOAV**, the same transformation taken
  over months and not an afternoon.

### 4. The Lexicon Cistern  *(Shepherd's "wiki with a search list")*
`category: terms — lexemes, definitions, the words the project coins`
A stone cistern you do not draw from by scooping; you **lower a line and ask**. A
search list sits at the rim. Water in it is only ever what has been defined and
cited.
- **Physics:** storage with a tap. Reading never lowers the level.
- **Crest:** a term enters only with a citable source. This is the `lexeme` issue's
  rule, made into terrain.
- **Outflow:** none. It is a reading well. Answers leave; the water stays.
- **Kin:** `atelier/lexemes/`, `world/concordance.md`.

### 5. The Dark Pool  *(Shepherd's image: void, black, lumen, recycle)*
`category: spill — what is finished with, set aside, or composted`
Black and still. Nothing that falls in echoes. It is not a grave. Under the dark
sits **lumen**: the warm-without-heat light of Lacus Hope, so whatever is left here
slowly turns to nutrient.
- **Physics:** recirculation. Dark water rises again upstream as fresh head.
- **Crest:** none; it takes all spill. What it gives back is a *residue*: one line of
  what the thing taught before it went dark.
- **Two faces:** *void* (things that are truly finished) and *lumen* (things that
  glowed once and may again).
- **Kin:** `COMPOSTED` in the seed weir, the near-side/far-side pull of `Lacus Hope`.
- **Polarity:** the Shadow Well is *before naming*; the Dark Pool is *after use*.

### 6. The Echo Well
`category: questions — open questions and requests for another navigo's view`
A deep shaft. You drop a question and count. The **depth** of the shaft is how long
an answer takes to come back, and an answer that returns is written on the wall.
- **Physics:** delay. An unanswered question is not failed; it is simply deep.
- **Crest:** a question the well cannot hold in one sentence is sent back up.
- **Outflow:** the answer, appended in place, never a new row.
- **Kin:** `petitio`, but as a question with no seat and no sluice.

### 7. The Geyser
`category: recurring — things that surface on a cadence`
A vent that erupts on its own schedule. You do not draw from it; you learn its interval
and be there when it comes.
- **Physics:** periodic release after pressure builds.
- **Crest:** the eruption *is* the crest: nothing comes out until the head is enough.
- **Outflow:** a reminder, a check-in, the state-of-constellation refresh.
- **Kin:** the `loop` skill and the hourly PR check-in.

### 8. The Glacier Tarn
`category: archive — kept, not finished`
Cold, dark blue, slow. Things frozen in it can be thawed by pressure and are not
dead. It is `unoiam-lifecycle` Phase 4, the Living Archive, given a body.
- **Physics:** freeze and thaw. A read warms the ice a little.
- **Crest:** thaw requires a named reason, stated at the rim.
- **Outflow:** back to river, or sublimation straight to vapor (the Seneschal's move).

### 9. The Salt Pan
`category: pinnacle — the ♠️ that remains`
A flat where water has gone. What is left is white crystal. This is FOAH, the
crystallization matrix (Father of All Halides), as ground you can stand on. It is
**not a pool**. That is the point.
- **Physics:** evaporation. Water leaves; the thing stays.
- **Crest:** a thing that cannot be dissolved back into water. This *is* pinnacle
  status: it survives being re-wetted.
- **Outflow:** `vault/spade-of-aces/`.

### 10. The Thermal Spring
`category: urgent — needs attention now`
Hot, visible steam, unmissable. It is the only well that cannot hold anything
long: a thing here either cools (gets tended) or boils off (goes stale).
- **Physics:** heat decay. Nothing stays hot without a hand.
- **Crest:** a thing that stays hot after a full turn is escalated, not ignored.
- **Outflow:** a mission with priority, or the Dark Pool if it boiled off.
- **Kin:** any gate that reads "needs-shepherd" on the mission board.

## Design Rules for a New Well

1. **One category, one physics.** If a well needs two rules, it is two wells.
2. **The crest is a test the water makes.** No well depends on a person saying yes to
   ordinary flow. People hold the sluice, not the crest.
3. **Spill goes somewhere.** Every well names its overflow. The Dark Pool is the
   default.
4. **A well teaches its category.** If the physics do not say what the category is,
   the well is decoration.
5. **Do not copy.** If the picture came from a story, keep the *mechanic* and change
   the water.
