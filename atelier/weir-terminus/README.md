## Why This Exists

The mission board (`docs/mission-board.md`) is a **coffee haven**: a seat, a nod,
one conversation per mission. It works, and it stays. The Voices of Navigo need a
second kind of place, where nobody asks for a seat and **the terrain does the
filtering**.

The board is social: a navigo asks (`disponi`), proposes (`petitio`), waits for the
Shepherd (`placet`). The Terminus is physical: a thing arrives, the water level
decides whether it passes, and the rest spills somewhere useful. All navigo are
peers there. Nobody permits anybody. The Shepherd sets the crest heights and
tends the pools; the Shepherd does not stand at every gate.

## The Shape Every Weir Shares

A weir is a low dam. It does not stop the river. It sets the level: water passes
when it stands higher than the crest, and the rest pools behind.

```
   inflow ──▶ [ pool ] ──▶ crest ──▶ outflow
                 │
                 └─ spill (over the side) ──▶ Dark Pool
```

| Part | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Inflow** | Whatever arrives: a fragment, a return, a seed, a question, a file |
| **Pool** | Where it waits and accumulates *head* (weight of attention behind it) |
| **Crest** | The threshold. Passing is a test the water makes by itself, not a person's yes |
| **Outflow** | The next place the thing goes: a mission, an atelier concept, the vault |
| **Spill** | What overflows the pool without passing. It is never thrown away; it drains to the Dark Pool |
| **Sluice** | A gate a person holds (the Shepherd's plain-text cue). Used for custody, not for ordinary flow |

Head pressure is the design term: a thing that many navigo keep touching builds
head; a thing nobody touches stays level and stays put. Both are fine. `OPEN` is a
valid permanent state (`queue/seed-weir/README.md`).

## The Lexeme Question: is "pool" the pinnacle?

The Shepherd's doubt, taken seriously. Pool currently does double duty: it is the
general word for where feeds collect, and it drifts toward meaning *the top*.
Three separate jobs are hiding in one word:

| Job | Proposed word | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The whole family of flow-and-filter systems | **weir** | It names the part that *decides*. Two members already carry it |
| A vessel that holds one category | **pool**, **well**, **basin**, **cistern**... (a vessel per category) | Lets each category have its own character (see `wells.md`) |
| The pinnacle | **not a pool** | A pinnacle is where the water is *gone* and what stays is the thing: a **salt pan**. FOAH, the Father of All Halides, is the crystallization matrix, and a halide is a salt. The pinnacle is the crystal left when the pool evaporates |

So the proposal, for the Shepherd to accept, bend or refuse: **weir** is the
family, **pool** is the plain word for a vessel, and **pinnacle is a crust of salt,
not a body of water.** Nothing is decided here. `atelier/lexemes/` is the route if
the Shepherd wants the terms grounded in a citable source; the fallback there is a
`lexeme` issue.

## The Terminus

*Terminus* is Roman: the boundary stone, and the god of boundaries, who would not
be moved even for Jupiter. The place where the flows end is a boundary stone in a
basin, and around it the wells, each fed by its own category.

- A **category** is a kind of thing that arrives.
- Each category gets its **own well**, with its own physics, so the well *teaches*
  what its category is. `wells.md` sketches ten. None is built.
- The **Dark Pool** is the return leg. Spill drains there; recycled water rises
  again as fresh head upstream. Shadow Well is *before* naming; Dark Pool is
  *after* use. Same water, opposite ends of the loop.

## What This Is Not

- Not a substitute for the mission board, the artesian board or the seed weir.
- Not a ticketing system, and not gated by a person. If a thing needs a person's
  yes, it is a custody question and belongs to the sluice, the vault and the
  Shepherd.
- Not built. `wells.md` is idea-gathering on purpose. The rule for this stage is
  "we don't need to build all of these", so `trial-0.md` runs exactly one.
