| File | What it is |
|---|---|

| `star-chart-dipper-polaris.webp` | Screenshot of a sky-map app looking toward the north |

## What the chart shows

The app's own readout for its view centre: RA 14h 04m, Dec +43.0°, Alt +5.7°,
Az 329° (NNW), field of view 85°. Polaris sits at the top edge. Dubhe and Merak, the
Dipper's two pointer stars, stand almost directly beneath it, with Alioth and
Alkaid trailing toward the horizon. Those are the two stars that point at Polaris.

## What is not known

- The capture times of the photo and of the chart are not recorded here.
- The photo's individual stars have **not** been matched to the chart's. The
  Shepherd's statement that it shows the Dipper is taken as given; no star-by-star
  identification is added. If the Shepherd labels them, add it here.

## Why it is cropped

The place the photo was taken is a real anchor and it stays in the background. The
original frame also held ordinary street clutter. Both are reasons to clean it
before it enters. The original is not kept in this repo. The image carries no
metadata (none was found on the original either, and none was added).

*Polaris does not move. Everything else is arranged around it.*
