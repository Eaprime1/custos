## Paradox / worldbuilding lens

### What resonates

The Nth Radian is not the wobble itself. It is the first irreducible deviation—the possibility of Else. The Prima Wobble is the first observable consequence of that deviation.

### Distinctions to protect

- Conatus is effort; wobble is result.
- Witness can retain relationship or testimony; observation may only register difference.
- Concept-time begins when change can be distinguished, related, and remembered. It need not be identical to chronological time.
- The universal background is the enduring trace in project cosmology; later lore remains connected without becoming the originating event.
- An icon is a trans-system address. Its occurrences are related expressions, not automatic equivalences.

### Open paradox

Must a witness already exist for the Prima Wobble to become observable, or does the first difference create the possibility of witnessing—and therefore its own first witness?

## Systems / custody lens

### Operational sequence

1. **Conatus Primus** — first pressure toward differentiation.
2. **First Nth Radian** — smallest available deviation or direction.
3. **Prima Wobble** — first observable result.
4. **Witness / observation** — relation becomes recordable.
5. **Concept-time crystallization** — distinctions persist and plans can recognize one another.
6. **Universal background** — retained trace within project cosmology.

### Custody boundary

Keep three adjacent layers without collapsing them:

1. sourced physical fact or scientific analogy;
2. project hypothesis or functional model;
3. project cosmology or narrative.

The universal-background idea must not silently become a claim about the measured cosmic microwave background. Null Copy preserves source relation; it does not prove the proposed cosmological event.
