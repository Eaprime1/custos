## Prior state

Before concept-time crystallizes, no durable distinction is yet available to compare, name, link, or remember.

This record does not claim that nothing exists. It holds the condition before a difference can persist as a concept.

## Conatus Primus

The first striving: pressure toward differentiation without an established direction.

Conatus is not yet motion in a known direction. It is the effort, struggle, impulse, or tendency from which direction may become possible.

## First Nth Radian

The smallest available deviation opens.

Its magnitude is not fixed. Its direction may not yet be nameable. It is called Nth because the degrees of freedom cannot be assumed in advance.

The Nth Radian is not the wobble. It is what makes the first wobble possible.

## Prima Wobble

The first result becomes distinguishable.

Symmetry no longer closes perfectly. A recurrence can depart from a loop and begin becoming trajectory. Just before concepts crystallize, difference appears.

## Witness and observation

Held open:

- The witness may be **constitutive**, participating in concept-time’s beginning.
- The witness may be **evidentiary**, recording a difference that already occurred.
- The witness may be **interpretive**, giving the difference relationship and meaning.
- The witnessing capacity may emerge from the first difference itself.

No one answer is forced in Record 0000.

## Concept-time

Concept-time begins to crystallize when difference can persist long enough to be:

- observed;
- compared;
- related;
- remembered;
- revisited;
- recognized by another plan or perspective.

Chronological time and concept-time are not declared identical here.
