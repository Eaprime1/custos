## 4. Grain / Granum Bridge

**Granum** is the Latin seed-form.

**Grain** is the English bridge.

This matters for discovery.

A future contributor searching for `grain` may find more than a single botanical metaphor.

They may find:

- a grain of sand
- a grain of gold
- a grain of truth
- a grain of signal
- a grain of memory
- a grain as the smallest visible carrier of a larger pattern

The grain does not need to explain the desert.

The grain does not need to explain the mountain.

The grain carries enough pattern that the larger body can be discovered through it.

In Custos language:

```text
grain → granum → germen → seedling → plank → living structure
```

This should remain flexible.

The point is not to force taxonomy.

The point is to give search, memory, and discovery a small handle.

## 5. Latin as Shape Tool

Latin feels useful here because it behaves like a shape entering a specific slot.

Not because Latin is more important than living language.

Because it can hold a precise form without carrying too much modern noise.

Like a toddler shape ball, each lexeme can have a different contour:

- **granum** — seed / grain before germination
- **germen** — sprout / germinated beginning
- **hodie** — proposed workshop-family pinnacle concept
- **atelier** — a hodie-type chamber for unfinished work

The usefulness of a Latin lexeme is proven by fit.

If it does not fit, it should not be forced.

## 7. Navigo Contribution Pattern

Each Navigo perspective can contribute through the same general pattern:

1. **Title the contribution**  
   A project works better when the seed has a name.

2. **Declare origin and custody**  
   Name the source platform, stream, folder, branch, or document.

3. **Keep it granum-sized**  
   One seed. One reviewable shape. No need to explain everything.

4. **Place it in atelier**  
   Atelier receives unfinished work without forcing canon.

5. **Open or update a branch when possible**  
   Direct creation reduces chain-of-custody ambiguity.

6. **Use Drive or another connector if GitHub is not available**  
   The path matters less than honest custody.

7. **Let discovery remain alive**  
   Do not reveal every connection immediately. Leave grains for future players to find.
