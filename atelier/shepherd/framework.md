## Layer Stack

```
LAYER 0: Creator-Nullus-Else
         Who holds the shepherd position determines everything downstream.

LAYER 1: Shepherd Principles (Operational)
         Provide | Guide | Restore | Protect | Rescue | Accompany
         Rod (defense from external threat)
         Staff (retrieval from self-induced danger)
         Two tools. Two failure modes. Know which applies.

LAYER 2: Sheep as Explorer
         Wandering = curiosity in motion. Not failure.
         Wool burden = vulnerability created by the relationship itself.
                       The same relationship that created it provides the care.
         Voice recognition = the sheep is an active participant in authentication.
         "Thy rod and staff comfort me" — the SHEEP is speaking.
                       Awareness sufficient to find comfort in tools before rescue needed.

LAYER 3: Authentication (John 10)
         Multi-herd pen: many flocks, one sheepfold, many shepherds calling at once.
         The sheep sort themselves by voice — no external verifier.
         Called by name — individual relationship, not category.
         Wolf test: the hired hand flees. The authentic shepherd stays.
         Flat earth and extremism = burned passive sonar.
                       Bad shepherd exposure breaks authentication over time.
                       Fix: rebuild relationship before broadcast. Not louder signals.

LAYER 4: Passive Sonar / Discord Management
         Receive, don't transmit. Build a picture from incoming signals.
         Classify discord — do not eliminate it:
           - Legitimate shepherd, wrong flock: ignore, no alarm
           - Wolf mimicking shepherd: threat signal
           - Background flock noise: filter
           - Your shepherd, distant: orient and move closer
           - Unknown voice: hold position, gather more data
         Adapt harmonies to hear through the noise.
         Then diagnose: is the discord a signal of something good, or a problem?

LAYER 5: Anti-Optimization / The One and the 99 (Luke 15)
         "Leave" = poor translation. kataleipō = leave in custody, not abandon.
         The 99 are in the sheepfold / with a guardian / with dogs. Not alone.
         The story is about the shepherd and the lost — not the 99.
         Why is the sheep lost? (triage before action)
           - Wandered (curiosity): go where curiosity leads
           - Predator/injury: threat assessment first — is the wolf still present?
         The 99 are sensors: they show the shepherd how and where the one went missing.
         Decision framework before going after the one:
           1. Is it known by name? (relationship required — not category)
           2. Can the 99 be held safely? (custody problem solvable?)
           3. Is it actually lost, or in a different pen? (classify before acting)
           4. Do you know what found looks like for this one?
         The rejoicing asymmetry: greater joy over the one found than 99 never lost.
         This breaks standard value accounting. It only works from relationship, not math.

LAYER 6: Error Analysis (Ezekiel 34)
         See: shepherd/ezekiel34.md — full failure catalog

LAYER 7: Get Along as Invariant
         613 mitzvot → 10 Commandments → 2 commands → 1: Get Along
         Unique attributes: fine. Shared operating condition: coexistence.
         The 613 were shepherd tools at civilization scale — rod and staff for a nation.
         Fat sheep problem: internal flock exploitation breaks the invariant.
         Legal precedent = fat sheep case law. Gold mine for error analysis.
```
