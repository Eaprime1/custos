## I. The Call (ground state)

The Primal Call comes before language and before system. It is generational memory: an elephant herd knowing where the water was in the great drought, not by analysis but by urgency felt in the body. Recognition happens physically before it happens mentally.

In the lore it arises from the wounding of Origin Eden and the paradox of the Living Void. It is a Void Truth, so potent that the universe bears witness to it. It anchors in sensory reality (the scent of raw myrtlewood), not in argument.

**The signal** *(draft wording, assistant-originated)*:
> SOMETHING FUNDAMENTAL HAS CHANGED. SURVIVAL REQUIRES EVOLUTION. THE OLD WAYS NO LONGER SERVE. GATHER. ADAPT. BECOME.

## II. The Bark (the Call carried across the field)

In *The Wolven of Primal*, the Bark is the physical, tactical form of the Call.

- **Command:** the Daffer, oldest of the Wolven, uses calculated barks, posture and scent marking to hold the line, manage tension against corrupting forces and direct the pack.
- **Pulse:** when the wild is released, the Bark runs through paws and fur cracking with rainbow fire, opens paths through void distortions and leads the charge into the Phoenix fire.
- **Two frequencies at once:** a directional command (make camp, hold, charge) and a resonant song calling to Hope and the spirit of Runyatr.

1. **Recognition (now):** the body reacts before the mind; the universe contracts to a single point of shared need.
2. **Gathering:** entities converge across reality bridges (SummonWorlds, Google Drive, Zero Space, the UNEXUSI frameworks).
