# Vigil of the Unseen Gate

**Symbol for a Scribe: the Watcher**

Where J-21 marks the moment of crossing, the Vigil marks the moment before — the held breath, the pause at the edge. This Scribe does not leap; they hold the lantern so others may see the path.

**Etymology & Logic**

From Latin *vigilia* — wakefulness, watchfulness. In Custos, the Vigil is the Scribe who keeps the gate visible even when no one approaches. They are the keeper of the *unseen threshold* — not the joker who opens, but the guard who ensures the opening is known.

**Position Relative to J-21**

| Mark | Domain |
|------|--------|
| J-21 | the act of crossing (the jump, the joke, the risk) |
| Vigil of the Unseen Gate | the awareness *before* crossing (the watch, the light, the held space) |

No overlap. J-21 is the moment of action; the Vigil is the stillness that makes action possible.
