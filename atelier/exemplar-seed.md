## The what

In the Shepherd's words, kept close: the Exemplar as a character, possibly in the idle adventures, that shows
up when something has an exemplary quality. A powerful lexeme that speaks volumes by its presence alone, and
as a descriptor of quality would carry high value or impact.

The pairing floated: the high end is the exemplary, one lexeme; the low end could be an antagonist
character. The Exemplar helps the antagonist (the issue, or some types of missions). A third character could
offer something that meets or exceeds the mission, with an option for content issues that might need an
exception.

## The cautions, carried forward

- **Rarity.** Do not dilute it by over-application. A mark that appears everywhere stops meaning anything.
- **Be mindful of absolutes.** The Shepherd's closing instruction.

## What is not decided

- A character sheet, a trigger rule, or any link to scoring (the grading in the heritage draft is a
  separate thing; this seed does not connect them).
- Whether it is one lexeme, a character, or both. The Shepherd said it was both; how they relate is open.
- Whether the antagonist and the third character are the same pair as any figure already in `world/`.
  Not checked here.

## Routing

It belongs with the lore and the idle adventures. Whether it becomes a lexeme (a term grounded in a
citable source, through `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/lexeme.yml`) is the Shepherd's call. Not filed as an issue.
The hand-off is in `atelier/heritage-pipeline/HANDOFFS.md`.
