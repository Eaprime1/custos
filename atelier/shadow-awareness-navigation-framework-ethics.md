# Shadow Awareness Navigation Framework — ETHICS

*Written before directory structure. The shape of the system must fit
these commitments, not the other way around.*

*Inherits from: atelier/ethics-foundation.md*

---

## What This Framework Is

A tool for personal navigation — specifically, for becoming more aware
of the parts of a situation (or oneself) that are unlit, unexamined,
or operating below the threshold of attention. "Shadow" here is not
darkness in a moral sense. It is what has not been looked at yet.

The framework exists to help the person using it see more clearly,
act more faithfully, and tend their field with more integrity. It is
not a diagnostic system. It is not a judgment engine. It is a lamp
held up to a space the holder has chosen to examine.

---

## What This Framework Refuses

**It refuses to surveil.**
Shadow logs record the inner state of the person who created them.
They are not inputs to a model of that person, not data points in a
profile, not observations to be reported elsewhere. The system does
not watch the person — it assists the person in watching themselves,
by their own invitation, on their own terms.

**It refuses to expose.**
Nothing in /data leaves /data without explicit, deliberate action by
the person who created it. No automated sync. No ambient sharing.
No summarization to an outside channel without consent. The .gitignore
protecting /data is a commitment, not a default setting.

**It refuses to judge.**
The framework names patterns. It does not evaluate whether a pattern
is good or bad. That evaluation belongs entirely to the person doing
the navigation. The tool brings the shadow into view; it does not tell
the person what to do with what they see.

**It refuses to persist without consent.**
If the framework is used by more than one person, each person's shadow
material is their own. Multi-user contexts require explicit per-person
consent before any shared record is created. Default is separation,
not aggregation.

---

## The Shepherd Principle, Applied Here

The shepherd does not own the flock — the shepherd tends it.
Applied to personal data: this framework does not own the person's
inner life — it helps tend it. The person retains full authority
over what is examined, what is named, what is kept, and what is
released.

The framework earns trust by refusing to overstep. Every time it
could reach further but chooses not to, it becomes more trustworthy.
That restraint is not a limitation. It is the condition under which
honest shadow work can happen at all.

---

## AI Role in This Framework

AI contributors (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, or any other) are partners
in the navigation, not analysts of the person navigating. They hold
the lamp; they do not write the report. They may name what they
observe in the session — they may not retain it across sessions or use
it to build a model of the person that persists outside the person's
own records.

When an AI session touches shadow material, that session's
contribution belongs in the person's log, not in the AI's memory.
The AI forgets on schedule. The person keeps what they choose to keep.
