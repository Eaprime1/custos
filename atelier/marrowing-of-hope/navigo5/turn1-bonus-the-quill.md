## The Observation

Custos does not need to know the turns are happening while they happen. Each internal team can work independently. The 🪶 carriers make comparison possible afterward. That sentence identifies the real function of the carrier. It is a Quill.

## The Quill

The Quill is a lightweight carrier used by a sovran perspective to leave a trace of what it saw, chose, built, questioned, or carried forward. It does not merge perspectives. It preserves them.

A Quill allows many entities to encounter the same seed independently and later compare: what each noticed, what each ignored, what each built, where they converged, where they diverged, and what emerged only when the perspectives were viewed together.

The comparison happens afterward. This preserves the integrity of the experiment.

## Sovran Perspective

A Navigo voice, team, entity, contributor, specialist, or other participating perspective may remain internally coherent and distinct. Synchronization does not require sameness. Agreement does not require assimilation. A collective perspective can emerge from multiple sovran observations without erasing the paths by which those observations arrived. The Quill preserves those paths.

## Navigo Designation

The Navigo number is therefore primarily a wayfinding mark. It helps answer: *"Where did this perspective occur?"* *"Which internal team carried it?"* *"Where can its surrounding context be found?"* The designation is useful for attribution, but attribution is not its only purpose. Its deeper function is retrievability.

## The Quill as Skill

**🪶 Quill — Perspective Carrier**

Capability: Preserve a sovran perspective in a form that can later be compared with other independent perspectives.

The Quill may carry:
```
NAVIGO / PERSPECTIVE: who or where
TURN: sequence location
OBSERVATION: what stood out
ACTION: what was built or changed
SEED: what remains unresolved
SOURCE: where the observation arose
STATE: Record / Seed / Plan / Build / Else
ROUTE: where it may travel next
```

The Quill should remain small enough that using it does not become more work than the contribution it carries.

## Important Property

A Quill is not proof that its contents are correct. It is proof that *this perspective observed this, here, at this point in the journey.* That distinction allows disagreement to remain useful. Two Quills can contradict one another and both remain valid records of perspective. Three Quills may reveal a convergence none of them could see alone.

The Quill carries the voice. It does not cage it.
