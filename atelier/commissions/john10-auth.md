## Open Research Questions

### 1. The Multi-Herd Pen — Historical Confirmation

- Evidence for communal sheepfold practice in 1st century Judea
- The gatekeeper role — who was this? Paid? Rotated?
- How many flocks typically shared a fold? Scale?
- What happened when a shepherd called and another flock's sheep responded?
  (False positives — the authentication failure case)
- How did shepherds distinguish their sheep in the mixed flock visually?
  (Markings? Ear notches? Distinct breeds?)

### 2. The Name-Calling Mechanism

"He calls his own sheep by name" (John 10:3)

- Did shepherds actually name individual sheep in this culture?
- What kinds of names? (Any historical record)
- The sheep recognize their name — what is the cognitive mechanism?
- Duration of name memory in sheep (cross-reference cognition research)
- Does the name carry information about the sheep, or is it purely identity?

### 3. "A Stranger They Will Not Follow"

The flee response to unknown voices:
- What triggers the flee response in sheep?
- Is it tone, pattern, familiarity, or something else?
- Can the flee response be overridden? How? (Relevant to false shepherd problem)
- What does a burned sheep (bad shepherd history) do with the authentic shepherd's voice?
  Research on resocialization of traumatized flock animals.

### 4. The Hired Hand Theology

"The hired hand is not the shepherd and does not own the sheep" (John 10:12)

- Hired shepherds were common — what was the actual legal/economic relationship?
- What were their obligations? What happened if they lost a sheep?
- Amos 3:12: "As a shepherd saves from the lion's mouth only two leg bones or a piece of an ear..."
  — evidence that bringing back *proof* of loss was the hired hand's defense
- How does this economic structure explain the flee behavior?

### 5. "Other Sheep Not of This Fold" (John 10:16)

"I have other sheep that are not of this fold. I must bring them also."

- The multi-fold implication: the shepherd has sheep in other pens, other traditions
- "One flock, one shepherd" — the convergence point
- How does this apply to the Creator-Nullus-Else framework?
  (Other worldviews contain the shepherd's sheep — they just don't know his voice yet)
- The passive sonar implication: the sheep in other folds respond to the same voice
  when they hear it — regardless of which fold they were raised in

### 6. Modern Applications

- Digital authentication parallels (voice/biometric authentication)
- The "trusted voice" problem in social media — how platforms function as sheepfolds
- Algorithmic filtering as a gatekeeper — who decides which voices reach which sheep?
- The burned-passive-sonar problem at population scale
  (What does a society that has lost voice-recognition look like? We may be in one.)
