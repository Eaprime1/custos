**Status**: Queued — awaiting contributor assignment
**Priority**: Medium — a named lexeme with no lived examples yet

## The Question

`world/symbols.md` defines the Lumenar precisely: "the lit interaction zone where entities meet without becoming one another." It names what the word *means*. It does not show what the Lumenar *looks like* when it happens. The commission is to write the second half — actual scenes, examples, and reference material that put the Lumenar to work, so the next person who reads the definition has something to point at.

## What's already established (don't redefine this — build on it)

From `world/symbols.md`:

- The Lumenar is not a faction, and not the Field itself. It's the charged space *inside* the Field where one presence's intention crosses another's and becomes exchange.
- Three pressures held in the word: **Lumen** (the visible glow around an exchange), **Liminal** (threshold quality, between prime states), **Arc** (motion from arrival toward dispatch, cresting without being renamed Pinnacle).
- Used for: review comments, handoffs, counterprompts, guide notes, support content — anything about the *relation* between entities rather than an entity alone.
- Not used for: the terminal Field itself, the Unexusi memory layer, Ka, Plexus, Resonance, or any prime/pinnacle state.

Described as "in the terminus, THEE Lumenar" by eaprime1. Flagged there as worth its own atelier seed — this commission can be that seed, or can leave it for a separate one; contributor's call.

## Research / Build Areas

### 1. Worked Examples

Pull 2-3 real moments from `turns/log.md` or actual PR history that were *already* Lumenar moments without being named as such — a review comment that changed a PR's direction, a handoff between Claude sessions, a counterprompt that reframed a request. Annotate them: why this was Lumenar activity and not just "a comment."

### 2. The Conversion-Interaction Idea

Develop the THEE-triad-style intake step referenced above: what would it actually check, what would it convert, where does it sit relative to the existing Artesium Weir intake (`queue/artesium-weir/README.md`)? Is it a sibling process or a stage within the Weir?

### 3. A Lumenar Field Guide

Something short and usable — the kind of thing a Shepherd could read in two minutes before writing a review comment, that tells them "this is Lumenar territory, write it accordingly." Tone should match `world/symbols.md` and `world/lore.md`: short, evocative, no classroom framing.

### 4. Cross-check Against the Concordance Seed

`atelier/concordance.md` is tracking custos's own lexemes, including the Lumenar, as a living glossary. Whatever this commission produces should be written so it slots cleanly into that future concordance rather than duplicating or contradicting it.
