## Research Areas

### 1. Pinnacle Branch — Origin and Domestication

- Earliest evidence of sheep domestication (current estimate: ~10,000 BCE, Mesopotamia)
- Wild ancestor: Ovis orientalis (mouflon) — current range, characteristics
- The domestication event: how, where, with whom
- Geographic overlap with early Hebrew narrative (same region, same timeframe)
- Earliest sheep in historical record — art, text, bone record
- How many independent domestication events? (May be more than one)

### 2. Cognitive Research — Current Science

What do we actually know about sheep intelligence?

- Face recognition: sheep recognize individual human and sheep faces for years
  (Cambridge University research — comparable to primates in some studies)
- Name recognition: what is the mechanism? How long does it persist?
- Emotional states: sheep show measurable emotional responses to faces
- Social memory: flock hierarchy, individual relationship tracking
- Stress response: the "lose it" behavior — what triggers it, what it reveals
  (Run from own shadow. Mind blind. Stress cascade to exhaustion/death.)
- Comparison to canine cognition — where parallel, where different

### 3. Breed Diversity

- How many recognized breeds? (hundreds globally)
- Spectrum from mouflon-close to extreme domestication
- Breeds that semi-shed (Soay, Icelandic) vs. continuous wool (Merino)
- Temperament variation across breeds
- Terrain adaptation — mountain breeds vs. lowland breeds vs. desert breeds
- The Soay sheep of St. Kilda: isolated flock, no shepherd for decades — what happened?
  (This is a natural experiment in sheep without relationship)
- Breeds approaching extinction — what are we losing?

### 4. Complete Value Inventory (Carver Principle)

Expand on sheep/complete-value.md:
- Pharmaceutical applications (current and potential)
- Gut microbiome research status
- Wool applications beyond textiles
- Sheep in carbon sequestration research
- Behavioral algorithm contributions (Boids and descendants)
- Sheep in contemporary art, therapy, education
- Economic data: global wool market, meat market, milk market
- The shofar — history, protocol, current use

### 5. The Three Shepherd-Figures

Deep dive on David, Jacob, Moses as shepherd-archetypes:
- David: what songs did he sing? What is known about his shepherd practice?
  What species of predators did he face in Judean terrain?
- Jacob: the Laban story in full — the selective breeding account (Genesis 30)
  is one of the earliest recorded genetics experiments. What did Jacob know?
- Moses: from prince to shepherd to liberator. What did the Midian years change in him?
  Jethro's flock — what kind of terrain, what practices?

### 6. Historical Rights and Value Systems

- How have different cultures valued sheep beyond food/wool?
- The sacrifice system: what were the quality standards? What did "without blemish" mean practically?
- The sacrificial system as food safety infrastructure — more evidence
- Sheep in Roman practice vs. Hebrew practice vs. Greek practice
- First recorded sheep rights argument (if any)
- The Oregon ballot measure context — what's the actual legal text?
