# COMMISSION: GREENACRES, OREGON — PLACE LORE FOR THE HOUSE OF CONFUSION ANCHOR

**Status**: Queued — awaiting contributor assignment
**Priority**: Medium — anchors the House of Confusion in a real place without exposing real people

---

## The Question

The House of Confusion (`world/unoiam-lifecycle.md` Phase 0, and the still-unplaced HOC architecture document from this session's intake) is anchored to a real location: Greenacres, a neighborhood in District 9, on the upper slough of the Coos River, near Eastside Elementary, Millicoma Junior High, and Marshfield High School — all real places in the Coos Bay, Oregon area. A house sat up the hill there, weather-worn blue, looking down over the valley. It's gone now — repainted, then gone.

The commission: build out Greenacres as a *place*, not as anyone's biography. Geography, water, weather, light — the things a location has regardless of who lived in it. The goal is a location anchor solid enough that anyone who actually grew up there would recognize it, without naming or describing any specific real person.

## Research Areas

### 1. The Geography

- The Coos River system — main stem, forks, the slough structure specifically (sloughs are tidally influenced, which matters for a "water failsafe" lore connection)
- Where Greenacres sits relative to the river, the bay, and downtown Coos Bay/North Bend
- Elevation and hillside orientation — what a house "up the hill, looking down over the valley" would actually see: which direction, what's visible, what the light does at different times of day
- The estuary ecology — what lives in an upper slough that wouldn't live further out in the bay

### 2. The Built Landscape, Era-Appropriate

- District 9 (Coos Bay School District) — what schools fed into Marshfield High, and in what order a kid would pass through them (Eastside Elementary → Millicoma Junior High → Marshfield High is the given sequence)
- What that part of Coos Bay looked like architecturally in the relevant decades — house styles, paint conventions ("weather-worn blue, the kind that doesn't quite hold a name" is already established lore; don't contradict it, build around it)
- The timber/fishing economy's visible footprint on the area — mills, docks, the things a kid would have grown up walking past without thinking about them

### 3. Weather and Water, Tied Back to the Lore

`world/unoiam-lifecycle.md` makes water the governing medium for state change. Coos Bay has real, specific weather: coastal fog, the rain patterns, king tides in the slough. The commission should surface real meteorological/tidal detail that could be borrowed for the lore's "weather governs the system" mechanic (`Custos_Proposal__Hydrologic_StateChange_Architecture.md`, Section IV) — without inventing fictional weather where real weather already does the job.

### 4. The "Disorientation as Design" Angle

The House of Confusion lore frames itself as intentionally disorienting — "you walk in expecting not to follow it, and that's exactly right." Research whether anything about the real Greenacres terrain (hillside layout, slough-cut roads, dead ends, a town built around water rather than a grid) naturally supports that framing, so the metaphor is built on real terrain rather than invented on top of it.

## What NOT to do

- Do not invent biographical detail about any real person connected to this place. No names, no personality, no specific memories that aren't already given in the source material.
- Do not treat this as a tribute or memorial page. It's a location anchor for lore — solid enough to recognize, not a story about anyone.
- If research surfaces information about a specific living person, leave it out and flag it rather than including it.

## Deliverable Format

- `atelier/greenacres/geography.md` — river, slough, hillside, light
- `atelier/greenacres/built-landscape.md` — schools, houses, era-appropriate detail
- `atelier/greenacres/weather-and-water.md` — the real meteorology/tides, with explicit pointers to where each could feed the hydrologic lore mechanic
- Flag anything that should route into `world/unoiam-lifecycle.md` or a future House of Confusion world-file, but don't edit those files directly from this commission — land findings here first.
