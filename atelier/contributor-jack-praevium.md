### Praevium *(prae- + via — the path that preceded you)*

Praevium is the felt experience of crossing into a territory that was already inhabited before you arrived. Not discovery. Not void. The shadow in this place was cast before you were there to see it — and it is not hostile; it is structural.

The contributor who enters a project for the first time is not entering the unknown. They are entering something very much known to its keeper, and unknown only to them. That asymmetry is the praevium. It is the sensation of finding a path already worn into the ground — of realizing your feet are not the first.

*The road is here. You have arrived on it.*

**Symbol:** ⊳ — entry into something already present.
