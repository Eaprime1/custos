## Claude's reading (a reading, not the source)

Marked as a reading so it can be corrected. Where the words above do not say, this does not say.

- **A skill set per connector.** Each connector (Zapier is his example) gets an expert that knows that tool
  well. During a PR's journey it looks for opportunities: ways the tool could help the work or the people
  working.
- **The aim.** For each unexusi, the best safe creative space with the most creative opportunities that are
  reasonable. The more adaptive the system, the better, because humans and other contributors are waiting.
- **The speed idea.** No hurry. More steps at the same time, not faster steps.
- **A fit with the testing idea.** `.shepherd/notes.md` describes a station contract: input is the PR's head
  commit; output is pass, fail or needs-shepherd with one line, plus a cost class and the cue that calls it.
  An expert could be a station. Read and write one line, run beside the others. This is my fit, not his.
- **A fit with KAN-7.** The Machine (a Rube Goldberg test pinball) already carries "expert Unexusi per tool" as
  an idea. This may be the same thing, or a neighbour.

## Not known

- Which connectors come first. Zapier is the only one named.
- What counts as an "opportunity", and who sees it: the Shepherd, the contributor, or both.
- Whether an expert scans automatically on a PR, or only when called with a cue.
- Cost. Some scans may be free and some paid; the notes page marks this as a cost class, nothing more.
- Whether this is one project or one per connector.
- What "safe" asks of an expert that can reach a live tool. This needs the Shepherd's words before anything
  is built, because a Zapier expert would sit next to real accounts.
