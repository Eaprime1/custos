## Formula (standard physics, not from the thread's author)

A flywheel stores rotational kinetic energy:

`E = ½ I ω²`

`I` is the moment of inertia and `ω` the angular speed. The formula says how much energy is
stored. It does not say whether the stored energy does any work. That is the Shepherd's point
below.

- **Slick flywheel.** One with almost no friction. Spinning is easy and doing something
  productive is impossible. It is motion without traction on reality.

  ingratiating speech that does not match the natural meaning of the words; also, containing
  an unusual amount of grease or oil.
- **Healthy oils.** A normal, needed exchange between entities. The Shepherd names hair, body,
  tears and sweat as oils that matter, each with its own balance.
- **System fit.** The same oil is useful in the right system and deadly in the wrong one. His
  example is motor oil in the brake fluid, power steering, radiator or washer reservoir.
