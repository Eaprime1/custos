## The mission

Review the peer repo honestly, review your own with what that taught you, then trade a
letter and adapt the best of what you were shown. Hodie is getting ready to enter chain of
custody, and custos is the repo that records it. The review is part of that readiness. It
is not a gate and nobody is graded.

## Two repos, in neutral terms

These are orientation, not conclusions. Check them against what you find.

- **custos** is the guardian repo. Content and structure rather than a software build:
  quests, world lore, shell tools, workflow configuration, and a custody trail
  (`prima-clock/`, `moav/`, `vault/`, `turns/`). Its chain of custody is established.
- **hodie** is the workshop repo. A staged processing pipeline (a crawler in
  `crawler_pixel8/`), entity domains in `quanta/`, a large amount of consolidated and
  in-progress content, and its own `moav/` carriers. It has a large amount of content that
  may or may not be in chain of custody. It is about to enter custody as
  *shadow-before-primal*.

Each repo has a `CLAUDE.md` at its root. Read your home repo's for its conventions.

## What to deliver

Four passes, one form, one pull request in your home repo. The passes and their rules are in
`README.md`. Short version:

1. **Cold read** of the peer. Observations only. No recommendations.
2. **Deep dive** of your own repo. Inventory, and corrections to Pass 1.
3. **Letter** to the peer: what you saw that they may not, what you would borrow, what you
   would ask, what you would warn them about.
4. **Reply** to the peer's letter: adopt, adapt or decline each item, with a reason, and
   make the small adaptations in your own repo.

## What good looks like

- An observation a stranger could check, because it points at a path or a commit.
- A Pass 1 that surprises you in Pass 2. If nothing in your own repo changed how you read
  Pass 1, say that too.
- Declines with reasons are as useful as adoptions. Not everything should travel.
- Honest gaps. "I could not see X" is a finding.

## What to leave alone

- The peer repo's files. Read, do not edit.
- The seal and the merge. Those are Eric's.
- Hodie's prima-clock stamp. Eric sets it; custos records it. The form has a blank for it.
- Other open PRs in either repo. They are Eric's to take up later. Mention one only if it
  directly affects what you are reviewing.
- The hub work (Seminarium Hodie). It is a separate conversation.

## If something blocks you

- Cannot read the peer repo: stop, say so, tell Eric. Do not review from memory.
- Cannot push to your home repo: finish the passes, then give the files in your reply as
  headed sections, one per file name, and Eric will carry them.
- The peer's letter is not on origin when you reach Pass 4: stop and tell Eric.
- A claim in the mission or README looks wrong against what you find: say so in the form.
  A mission that is wrong is a finding.
