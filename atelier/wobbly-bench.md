
# The Wobbly Bench — atelier seed

## The Idea

A tentative plan for how atelier itself should start, raised by eaprime1
on PR #41: build custos's own atelier pattern first, get it right here,
then — once happy with it — take it to the prima repo as the canonical
form. Custos is the proving ground, prima is the destination.

The starter atelier eaprime1 describes is deliberately simple and
deliberately a little broken: "a workbench with different length legs,"
or some other minor, solvable challenge baked into the first tool rather
than engineered out of it. Not a bug to fix before shipping — a feature
of the starting point.

The reason: a wobbly bench is informative. Watching how a user works on
one — their frustration level, the quality of what they produce, the
mistakes they make — tells you something a perfectly level bench never
would. The friction is the instrument.

## Why now

This surfaced directly in PR #41's review thread, alongside the
concordance seed, as eaprime1's framing for how atelier should bootstrap
itself: not by designing a polished first workbench, but by starting
with something simple enough to build quickly and imperfect enough to
be a minor test of whoever uses it.
