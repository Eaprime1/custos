## Part II — What's Actually Grown

**`Lumenar` has a worked example now, not just a definition.**
`world/symbols.md` named the word precisely: the lit interaction zone where two presences meet without becoming one another. It didn't show what that looks like in practice. A commission now exists (`atelier/commissions/lumenar-content.md`) asking for exactly that — real scenes pulled from this project's own history, a short field guide, and a look at a "conversion-interaction" idea raised in passing since PR #38 landed. Nothing in it changes what `Lumenar` means. It's the first attempt at showing it.

**`J-21`'s underlying math is still unnamed — flagged, not solved.**
The card arithmetic behind the symbol (`J - 21 = A`, `J + A = 21`) was noticed and written down (`incoming/pre-nullus/202606210052_J21-Lumenar-growth-observation.md`) as something worth naming someday. It hasn't been. That's an honest gap, not a quiet one — it's listed as open work in `atelier/commissions/message-to-the-commissioner.md`, the same place every other unfinished thread in this project gets tracked so it doesn't disappear.

**A role this project didn't have is starting to take shape.**
The Mulberry Initialization named five roles — Commissioner, Artesian, Quartermaster, Herald, Broker — without ever building any of them. `atelier/commissions/message-to-the-commissioner.md` is a letter written *to* the first of those roles before it exists, indexing every piece of work currently available to hand out. PR #38 is part of why that page has anything on it at all: it's the first proof this project could receive real, unsolicited contribution from outside itself, which is the whole premise a Commissioner role exists to serve.

**A separate role — the Seneschal — landed two independent drafts at once, and that became its own small lesson.**
While a commission asking "what does the Seneschal do" was mid-draft, the project owner posted two real answers to that exact question, written without seeing the commission or each other. Rather than pick one, the commission was rewritten to reconcile both — a deliberate small echo of the same thing this document is about: something arriving from outside the frame you were holding, and the frame changing to fit it rather than discarding what arrived.
