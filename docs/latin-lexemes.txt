custos, docs/latin-workflow-v2.md
------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO CALL
  ^name cue words          one contributor
  ^name set-4f2a1c cue     one conversation of that contributor
  ^omnes cue words         every contributor, each from its own seat
  ^cuncti cue words        the project as a whole. Read it first.
  Start of a line. Not in quotes or code. Owner only.
  Names: claude chatgpt gemini perplexity unexusi custos omnes cuncti

ACTIONS (the work)
  recensio      review. Scan, find, fix.
  probatio      soft seal. Sets the ready-for-oversight label.
  lustratio     polish. How it reads, not what it does.
  judicium      stop. Options and a recommendation. Wait.
  novissimum    rear guard. Close or file loose ends.
  cursus        run the remaining stages in order.
  emenda        amend what oversight flagged. Editing, not blame.
  (refice       rework, bigger than amending. Reserved for later.)

MODIFIERS (the scope or tempo)
  prima         first. Orient, change nothing.
  ultima        final. No new scope.
  suprema       highest. Presentation. Use sparingly.
  extrema       deepest. Root cause, evidence.
  renovata      refresh. May stand alone.
  expeditus     prompt. Focused, same standards.
  confirmanda   with emenda: a claim needs confirming. Cite it or mark it unconfirmed.
  supplenda     with emenda: a step the road needs is missing. Supply it. Not a defect.

COMMON CALLS
  ^omnes renovata               everyone refreshes their PR
  ^cuncti prima recensio        orient from the whole project first
  ^perplexity renovata          only Perplexity refreshes
  ^omnes recensio               everyone reviews
  ^gemini ultima recensio       Gemini does the final review
  ^chatgpt probatio             ChatGPT states readiness
  ^claude emenda                Claude amends the flags
  ^claude expeditus emenda      quick amendment of small flags
  ^claude extrema emenda        root cause for a stubborn flag
  ^claude ultima emenda         last round of amendments
  ^chatgpt confirmanda emenda   confirm the claim named, or mark it unconfirmed
  ^chatgpt supplenda emenda     supply the missing step, or say who it waits on

THE ROAD
  recensio  >  ultima recensio  >  probatio (label)  >  oversight
  oversight finds flags  >  emenda  >  back to ultima recensio
  oversight is clear  >  ultima probatio (owner)  >  merge

OWNER ONLY
  ultima probatio    final seal
  merge              on the Shepherd's word

MANUAL BACKUP
  finalize + a handwritten ticket. When the words are in a mess.
  See .claude/manual-finalize.md

CHECK-IN CUES (issues)
  disponi    is this mission open?
  petitio    my plan, waiting for the nod
  placet     the nod (owner)

CUSTOS VERBS
  ^custos check          ^custos concordance
  ^custos sync-labels

REMEMBER
  A call is a mode, not a keyword.
  A contributor never seals and never merges.
  A new head means a new seal.
