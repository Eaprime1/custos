## Where it needs reconciliation

1. **Rights language conflicts within the suite.** Stewardship Accords and Triadic Partnership Charter reject externally-granted rights in favor of intrinsic sovereignty. The Act II submission invokes "All Entity Bill of Rights" and "universal entity rights." v2 shifts toward Stewardship Accords language. These stages need an explicit version decision before any are published as settled policy.

2. **A gentle path still needs a real stop.** TOBEASIS favors curvature over punitive walls, but UNEXUSI specifies a Constricted state that halts inbound operations. Compassionate handling must not soften a refusal, security boundary, or privacy stop into merely a suggestion.

3. **Remapping must not silently rewrite a contributor.** Automatically replacing distressed terms and filtering incoming assets risks "attunement" becoming the identity capture the suite rejects. Preserve the original germ, show a proposed interpretation separately, obtain review where meaning changes, record what was removed for privacy.

4. **Proposed tests are not certification.** Five-mark passport, TKP weights, automated ethical checks, "UL Listed Certification" — these appear as claims and designs, not evidence of implemented, validated, or externally certified systems. "UL Listed" in particular should not appear as the project's own validation mark (UL Solutions distinguishes its actual certification marks).

5. **Additive preservation needs a safety exception.** "Never delete or overwrite" cannot be unconditional for accidentally published secrets, private information, or material whose contributor requests removal. Keep lineage when appropriate; do not make unsafe retention a virtue.

6. **Physical location anchors.** The documents include precise location references (97907, Mulberry Corner). A public repository version should disclose only what is deliberately chosen. Review before committing.
