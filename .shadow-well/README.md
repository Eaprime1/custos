## What This Is

`.artesian/` springs from structure — formal open missions, claim carriers,
defined arcs. The shadow well springs from the opposite: **nowhere**. Raw,
unlocated, unvetted material that has not yet been named, claimed, or routed
through any custody process.

The polarity: `now_here ↔ nowhere`

- `now_here` — present, located, in custody, named
- `nowhere` — not yet anywhere, not yet located, pure source material

The shadow well holds what has passed through the operator's field but has
not yet found its place. It is not `atelier/` (which names things and
develops them). It is pre-name. Pre-route. Pure emergent pressure from the
authentic stream.

## What Belongs Here

- Fragments that surfaced during a session but didn't fit the current arc
- Seeds from the personal stream (navigo2, the inbox) before vetting
- Concepts with no name yet — or whose name is still wrong
- The shadow version of an idea that already has a formal entry somewhere else
- Anything the Shepherd wants to hold without yet deciding what to do with it

## What Doesn't Belong Here

- Named, developed concepts (those go to `atelier/`)
- Open missions ready to be claimed (those go to `.artesian/`)
- Finished vault entries (those go to `vault/`)
- Queue items with a known destination (those go to `queue/`)

## Polarity Note

`now_here` and `nowhere` are the same letters, different spacing.
What is nowhere right now may become now_here when the Shepherd names it.
The shadow well is the waiting state between them.

## Form (decided 202609251844, issue #330)

**Near-formless on purpose.** An entry is one `.md` file with a prima-clock line and the word `nowhere`. There's no schema beyond that. Formlessness is the point, but an unstamped fragment can't be found again.

## Routing

The trigger is **the Shepherd names it**, nothing else. Once named:
- still developing → `atelier/`
- ready to be claimed → `.artesian/`
- a word worth keeping → `world/concordance.md`

The axis itself lives in world lore: `world/polarity.md`.
