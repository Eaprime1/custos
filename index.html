ᚷ The Gate

How to engage

Contributors enter through GitHub Issues. Three types of work are commissioned. Claim one by commenting claiming this and opening a PR.

Mission — clear deliverable, bash completion check
A mission has a defined outcome and an exact shell command to verify it. The Shepherd sets the target. The contributor chooses the path. Reviewers: eaprime1 (Shepherd / Deck Master).
Bounty — problem defined, approach open
The problem is named. The path is yours to propose. Bounties invite creative engagement with open questions in the custos world. The Shepherd reserves the right to redirect.
Upgrade — target exists, contributor sharpens it
A specific existing file or system is the target. The Shepherd has identified it for extension or refinement. Upgrades are the most constrained form of contribution — and the sharpest practice.
