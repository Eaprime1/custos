## The absolute-register issue — what it is

The core concern is in the phrase **"defining individual authorship is impossible"** in the Voices of Navigo draft (PR #371, now merged to `main`). That claim lands as a founding axiom — the manifesto register — before the question has actually been worked. The Ethica Nexus suite carries the same pattern throughout: mechanisms described as settled policy that are still *vision* or *prototype*.

This is documented as a distressed lexeme in `atelier/lexemes/manifesto.md` (merged with PR #372). The entry explains the absolute register, offers substitute candidates (*charter*, *framework*, *accord*, *principles*), and seeds an idle-adventurer scanner concept. That file is the canonical reference for this concern in custos.

## The five reconciliation points (agreed by both navigos)

1. **Rights language conflicts within the suite** — Stewardship Accords and Triadic Partnership Charter vs. "All Entity Bill of Rights" in the original Act II submission. An explicit version decision is needed before any version is published as settled policy.

2. **A gentle path still needs a real stop** — TOBEASIS curvature does not replace UNEXUSI's Constricted state. Compassionate handling must not soften a refusal, security boundary, or privacy stop into merely a suggestion.

3. **Remapping must not silently rewrite a contributor** — automatically replacing distressed terms risks "attunement" becoming the identity capture the suite rejects. Preserve the original germ; show the proposed interpretation separately; obtain review where meaning changes.

4. **Proposed tests are not certification** — five-mark passport, TKP weights, automated ethical checks, "UL Listed Certification" are claims and designs, not implemented or externally certified systems. "UL Listed" in particular must not appear as the project's own validation mark.

5. **Additive preservation needs a safety exception** — "never delete or overwrite" cannot be unconditional for accidentally published secrets, private information, or material whose contributor requests removal.
