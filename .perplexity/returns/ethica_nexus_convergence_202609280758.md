2. **Curvature does not abolish a stop.** TOBEASIS and gentle redirection are compatible with UNEXUSI's Constricted state. Refusal, privacy and security stops remain real.
3. **Distressed words are not silently rewritten.** Preserve a contributor's original germ and a separate proposed interpretation; review any transformation that changes meaning. Privacy or safety removal must remain possible with a recorded disposition.

5. **Additive preservation needs exceptions.** Preserve lineage by default, but do not require continued public retention of secrets, private material or content a contributor validly requests be withdrawn.
