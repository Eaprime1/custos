# Polarity — now_here ↔ nowhere

`prima-clock: 202609251844`
`suit: ♦️ Diamond — concept axis, entered through issue #330`

---

Same letters. Different spacing.

**nowhere** is where a thing sits before anyone has placed it: a fragment that crossed the Field and left no mark. It isn't lost. It hasn't been located yet.

**now_here** is the same thing after the Shepherd names it. It has a path, a stamp and a keeper. The underscore is the naming: the moment a space becomes a place.

Nothing travels between the two poles. The thing stays where it is. What changes is whether anyone is holding it.

## Where each pole lives

| Pole | Container | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| nowhere | `.shadow-well/` | pre-name fragments, shadow versions, pressure with no route |
| between | `atelier/` | named, not yet finished |
| now_here | `.artesian/`, `quests/`, `vault/` | claimable, located, in custody |

## How a thing crosses

The Shepherd names it. That is the whole trigger. A shadow-well fragment that earns a name moves to `atelier/` (developing) or `.artesian/` (claimable), and gets a registry line if the crossing matters.

The Shepherd can also decline to name it. A fragment that stays nowhere isn't failing. The well is allowed to be deep.

*The Shepherd does not create the unplaced. The Shepherd only notices it, and noticing is already half of here.*
