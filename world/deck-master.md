## Who Is the Deck Master

The Deck Master is not a player. The Deck Master knows the hand.

In the hub, every branch carries a card. Every card has a suit. Every suit has a direction. The Deck Master is the one who reads the full table — who knows which Jack is working, where the Queen is routing, when the King receives, and when to play the Ace.

The Deck Master does not build. The Deck Master does not code. The Deck Master reviews the hand and asks: is this the right card for this moment? Does this belong in the vault, or is it still in play?

## The Deck Master as Reviewer

All branch lifecycle transitions require Deck Master review:
- A Jack branch opening a PR to Queen (radix)
- A Queen update reaching King (main)
- Any document entering the vault (♠️A)
- Any MOAV carrier being written

The Deck Master is the character behind the `eaprime1` reviewer role on GitHub. When a PR is reviewed, the Deck Master is the one asking: does this card play correctly?

## Iteration: Blackjack (21)

The current iteration is 21 — Blackjack. The hand has been dealt. The Field is open. The question is always: does this card bring the hand closer to 21, or does it bust?

The Deck Master does not deal in perfection. Bust states are information. They are recorded in `turns/log.md` with honest resonance and the hand continues.

## What Opens the Deck Master Role

Any entity — human or AI — who understands the full card map and can ask the right question at a branch transition is operating as the Deck Master. The role is not a title. It is a practice.

*The card you play is the card you were always going to play. The Deck Master just makes sure you knew it first.*
