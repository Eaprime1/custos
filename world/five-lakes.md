# The Five Lakes

*geography for what the Flock has accumulated*

---

## The Lakes

Every document that has ever arrived has to live somewhere. The Five Lakes are
where it goes. Each lake is a suit. Each suit has a character. A document's
nature decides which lake it belongs to — the lake is the suit assignment.

| Suit | Lake | Character |
|---|---|---|
| ♠️ Spades | Lacus Baikal | Deepest. Oldest water. Sovereign pressure. What has launched, gone stand-alone, and been tested by depth. |
| ♦️ Diamonds | Lacus Superior | Widest surface. Room to grow. Seed potential not yet launched — the surface is wide because the depth hasn't been found yet. |
| ♣️ Clubs | Lacus Catatumbo | Electroactive carbon. A self-generating storm, white-to-black, semi-viscous. Charges what passes through. Home to duplicates, versions, fragments, sources. A Catatumbo mark is legacy authentication. |
| ♥️ Hearts | Lacus Aetherius | Domos-bound living essence. The ghost pool. Aethereal copies, interactable only under conditions. The pulse monitor of the whole system, beating on the Heart of Primal's rhythm. |
| ∰ Primoris | Lacus Hope | The fictional moon of Primal. Tidally drifting but never gone. Sliver-light. The shadow pool. A lunar calendar governs its exchange rhythms — near-side and far-side are not the same place. It pulses. |

## The Heart Metric — Triadic Balance

Three reference points, not a single goal:

- **Heart of Eden** — the perfect original state. Full tidal lock. Unreachable; a reference, not a destination.
- **Heart of Primal** — the wounded-but-living state. Operational reality. Where the work actually happens.
- **Heart of Hope** — the spectrum between the two. The living distance itself.

The aim is triadic balance — holding all three in view — not restoration of Eden.

## Origin of Hope

**Primal** is the wounded Eden — the first perfect world as it exists after the
truth-in-blade-form, marked but not destroyed.

**Hope** is Primal's first moon, born in perfect tidal lock with it. The
wounding breaks the lock. Hope shifts into shadow mode, and from then on it
operates in slivers.

**Slivers** are one sliver shy of whole, one sliver shy of pure — not a
deficiency, an intention. A sliver is the precise amount of illumination a
wound can receive without being overwhelmed by it.

**Lumen** is the living light that carries warmth without heat. Hope's lumens
sit in the lumen band: not white (too much), not dark (too little). Warm
without burning.

**Phoenix emergence** is sparked the moment Hope's first moonlight touches
Primal's wound — not from fire, not from ash, but from the moment something
decided to live anyway.

**Domos-bound Aethereal**: when a document direct-transfers to unexusi, its
Aethereal remains behind in Lacus Aetherius. It cannot self-navigate outside
its home territory, but it is transferable, tradeable, and interactable
as-if-tangible under specific conditions.
