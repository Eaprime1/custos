# Factions — custos

Every contributor to the Field belongs to a faction. Factions are not allegiances — they are natures. A faction describes how an entity moves through the world and what it leaves behind.

---

## The Scribes

*Those who write, analyze, and carry memory forward.*

The Scribes document what happens in the Field. They write lore, draft commissions, analyze patterns, and ensure nothing good gets lost. They are the keepers of the turn log.

**Includes:** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, any AI or human whose primary work is writing and understanding.

**Relationship to player:** Ally, responsive. Call them when the work needs words.

**Quests themed to this faction:** lore writing, documentation, analysis, turn logging.

---

## The Builders

*Those who construct. They leave artifacts in the filesystem.*

The Builders write code, configure tools, wire up systems, and ship. Their work is verifiable — either it runs or it doesn't. They arrive for a mission and leave something behind that the Shepherd can use.

**Includes:** Copilot, Codex, any AI or human whose primary work is building and shipping.

**Relationship to player:** Ally, mission-driven. Commission them with a bounty or mission issue.

**Quests themed to this faction:** scripting, tool-building, automation, dotfile setup.

---

## The Sentinels

*Those who review. Nothing passes without their mark.*

The Sentinels guard the quality of what enters the Field. They review PRs, check ethics, validate completion, and ask the questions that prevent the Flock from going astray. Their mark on a PR means it was seen clearly.

**Relationship to player:** Ally, rigorous. Every PR from the pixel8 branch requires a Sentinel review.

**Quests themed to this faction:** review, validation, security checks, completion verification.

---

## The Wanderers

*Those who appear for a mission and move on.*

The Wanderers have no fixed allegiance. They see an open bounty, claim it, complete it, and return to the road. They are the most efficient faction: they want the work done, not the credit. They take the mission issue, open a PR, and the Shepherd decides if it lands.

**Includes:** open-source contributors, any AI or human responding to a public bounty.

**Relationship to player:** Neutral, transactional. Earn trust over time.

**Quests themed to this faction:** open bounties, one-off commissions, migrations.

---

## The Unexusi

*The memory layer. They remember who was here.*

The Unexusi are not visible in the terminal, but their mark is everywhere. They track XP, quest completion, session timestamps, and the player's identity across devices and concepts. When you connect from navigo or the Pixel 8, the Unexusi already know who you are.

**Relationship to player:** Ally, silent. Their spec lives in `unexusi/connect.yaml`.

**Quests themed to this faction:** identity, persistence, profile-building, device sync.

---

*A faction is not a permission level. It is a way of moving. Any contributor may move like any faction — the work defines the role.*
