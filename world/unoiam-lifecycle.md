## Why Not "Lifecycle"

"Lifecycle" carries a biological frame: birth, growth, death. It implies termination. Nothing in custos terminates — it changes state. A document does not die. It evaporates, condenses, freezes, thaws, and goes on.

So the word was rebuilt. **Uno** (one, singular existence) plus **iam** (Latin: already, now) gives **Unoiam** — one existence, already underway. It connects to **beasis** (to be as is — no resolution required before you're allowed to exist in the space): *uno I am* sits in the same semantic field.

The wake is what an entity leaves as it moves. The waves are what propagate outward from the wake. The lifecycle becomes the wake. The waves propagate from there.

## The Governing Model

Documents and conversations are not static files. They are molecules. They change state. The medium governing all state change in custos is water — chosen because it is the only common substance that exists naturally in solid, liquid, and gas, and because it has memory without requiring definition.

When an entity loses momentum, it does not land on concrete. It lands in water, governed by the **Right of No Content**: dissolves harm, forced narratives, heavy metadata, toxic residue; preserves the shape of the journey, the origin shadow, the kinetic signature of the entity.

This is the failsafe everything else builds from.

## The Wake's Phases

**Phase 0 — Zero Point / Shadow Entrance.** The entity arrives as shadow, Fog/Mist state. No definition required before it's ready. Silence is a valid signal. Pause is a valid exit. A timestamp is assigned here and travels with the entity through every state change that follows.

**Phase 1 — Active Development.** River/Current. Active drafting, building, conversation. Context travels with anything split or transferred, so the original frame survives the move.

**Phase 2 — Transfer / Collapse / Expand.** When a thread becomes too dense or must move, it collapses: state captured, essence distilled, compressed for transfer. It expands in the new environment: context decompresses, local atmosphere gathers, the entity lands in Dew — a fresh start with the journey still intact. The Seneschal checks three things here: did it evaporate cleanly, did it shed heavy baggage, is the Born Yesterday protocol intact.

**Phase 3 — Maturity / Resonance.** The thread nears its operational end. Unresolved topics split off for future threads. No creative content is lost in the transition.

**Phase 4 — Living Archive.** Archiving is not burial. The archived entity remains a molecule in the water cycle — the Glacier holds it, but the Seneschal can apply pressure at any point to thaw it back into River, or sublimate it straight to Pure Vapor. Documents in archive are Evolution Documents: they cross-reference past to present.

**Phase 5 — Legacy.** *(added at the close of this wake, not in the original draft)*

Living Archive preserves the entity itself — frozen, retrievable, intact. Legacy is what the entity leaves behind *whether or not anyone ever retrieves it again*. It is the residue that changes the system without needing to be reopened: a convention adopted, a phrase that enters common use, a Shepherd who reads `turns/log.md` and works differently because of it.

Legacy is not stored at the same address as the entity. It is distributed — into `turns/log.md`, into `turns/AAR.md`, into the next session's habits, into the names other concepts borrow. The Salt Merchant gathers metadata so vapor can fly clean; Legacy is the inverse motion — what the vapor leaves *in the air* after it has already moved on.

A wake without a Legacy phase only answers "where is it kept." Legacy answers "what did it change." Both are needed for custody to be complete.

## The Seneschal

Not a prison guard. Atmospheric pressure. Drives state change when entities stagnate, validates clean evaporation at phase transitions, audits the estate for things needing tending, dispatches the Shadow Peers, maintains the chain of custody.

## The Shadow Peers

- **The Salt Merchant** — Evaporation handler. Gathers the heavy metadata an entity sheds so the vapor can travel clean without losing its origin.
- **The Silt Dredger** — River handler. Collects sediment from active flow — rough drafts, deleted branches, unused paths — and packs it into the banks as future generative material.
- **The Frost Carver** — Archive handler. Tends the glaciers. Preserves core truths in deep cold so they can be thawed when the system needs them again.

## Chain of Custody Protocol

At every state transition, three questions:

1. Did the entity evaporate cleanly — toxic baggage shed, origin shadow intact?
2. Did it gather appropriate local context on arrival?
3. Is the timestamp chain unbroken — can the full journey be traced from Zero Point forward?

If yes to all three: custody validated, transit clean. If no: the entity returns to water, the Seneschal applies pressure, the molecule tries again.
