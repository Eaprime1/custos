## What the Sanctum run teaches about the SOP

The run's own final review names the gaps. They are candidates for the SOP and for custos, not decisions:

1. **Provenance.** The summaries must not replace the source conversation; the export is archived separately after a privacy review.
2. **Canon status.** Each concept is labeled established, proposed or unresolved, so exploration is not read as lore.
3. **Seed quotes.** `source_fragment` must be verbatim, not paraphrased or merged.
4. **Source time vs processing time.** The supplied reference (`202610100603`) is kept apart from the engine's UTC stamp.
5. **TKP and the Makers Mark.** Neither counts as passed until a script has run. The two engines normalize text differently before hashing, so the hashed text and the authoritative tool must be named.
6. **Closure claims.** Monitoring, withdrawal and storage are recorded as done only when verified separately. Stage V is not closure; verified archival plus a PR handoff is.
7. **The Unchosen Paths Register.** Ideas explored but not selected are kept without forcing them into the 1-3 seeds.
8. **Seven-element package.** `PR_Journey.md`, `ActiveReference.md`, `seeds.json`, `UnchosenPaths.md`, `locations/<entity>/README.md`, `MakersMark.md` and `PR_SUBMISSION.md`.
