# The Seep Artois

*Where the artesian flow breaks surface.*

---

Artesian water needs no pump — it rises on its own pressure and finds the
surface on its own. The Seep Artois is where that happens in custos: carrier
pages that have crossed the Artesium Weir surface here and pool, ready to be
drawn.

## What Lives Here

Ready-to-develop pages. Each one is a carrier that has already been reviewed
at the Weir, has a plan, and is waiting for a Shepherd or contributor to draw
it into work — a quest, a mission, a bounty, an adventure.

## Natural Pressure One

The Seep flows at natural pressure: **one at a time**. Only one page may be
actively drawn (claimed, in progress) from the pool at once. This keeps the
pool honest — work doesn't pile up faster than it can be tended.

To draw from the pool:

1. Pick the page you'll work.
2. Mark it `status: drawn` in its front matter, with your handle and date.
3. When the work is dispatched, move the page on — to `vault/` history, a
   quest, a PR, wherever its plan points. Mark it `status: dispatched`.
