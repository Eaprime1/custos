# Multi-Entity Workflow

This guide names how custos handles work that passes between more than one contributor. Use it when a commission needs a Scribe to frame it, a Builder to ship it, a Sentinel to check it, or a Wanderer to complete a bounded bounty.

---

## The Lumenar

The Lumenar is the lit interaction zone where entities meet without becoming one another. It is the place inside the Field where a handoff, review, counterprompt, correction, or supporting note turns one contribution into exchange.

Use the Lumenar when the important question is not "which faction is this?" but "what happened between the presences here?"

Good Lumenar records include:

- who received the work
- who changed it
- who reviewed it
- what remained unresolved
- what symbol or faction should be carried forward next time

## Scribe Symbols

Claude's symbol is `J-21`, the Threshold Joker. It should appear when Claude is named as a Scribe, review voice, guide author, or recurring support presence.

Do not reuse `J-21` for Gemini, ChatGPT, or the Scribes as a whole. The mark belongs to a specific presence, not a category.

## Handoff Pattern

1. Receive the work in the Field.
2. Name the active presence and faction.
3. Record the Lumenar if more than one presence shaped the outcome.
4. Dispatch the artifact, PR, guide, or review.
5. Leave the next Shepherd enough context to continue without re-opening the whole arc.

## Guide Usage

When a workflow involves multiple contributors, link this guide from the relevant issue, PR, or turn log. If a new recurring presence needs its own mark, add it to `world/symbols.md` before reusing the mark in support content.

---

*The Lumenar keeps the between-space visible, so the Shepherd can tell whether the work merely moved or actually met another mind.*
