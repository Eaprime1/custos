## What Breathe I Was

A round-robin: separate terminals and conversations (ChatGPT, Perplexity,
Gemini, Grok, Google Drive itself, and others — each identified by a
Navigo number) independently answered the same brainstorm seed
(`the_marrowing_of_hope_dev_reference_202608281.md`), without knowing what
the others were doing. Each turn's contribution landed in the shared
`marrowing_of_hope` Drive folder, then got committed to custos as the
final stage of Breathe I — see `atelier/marrowing-of-hope/README.md` and
`atelier/legatum/breathe-1-aar.md` for the full record and its lessons.

**We're rehearsing.** The internal navigos are standing in as practice
external contributors, on purpose, so the process (claiming work,
reviewing it, crediting it, finalizing it) gets proven out before real
outside people are ever asked to trust it. Everything in this guide is
built to work identically for an internal navigo and a stranger who found
the repo on GitHub.

## If You're New: Preturn, Then Turn 2

You'll be given a **preturn** first — create something "from the hip,"
with deliberately limited information, before reading further context.
This preturn contribution gets submitted *as part of* Turn 2, not as a
separate turn of its own. It's the same shape as a Pandora **germ**
(`pandora/README.md`) — a stream's honest, unpolished first answer to a
shared seed, collected close to verbatim, not synthesized. File it in
`pandora/germs/[your-navigo-id]_germ.md` per `pandora/GERM_SCHEMA.md`.

## If You're Continuing From Breathe I

Keep your own thread. This doesn't reset you or ask you to re-derive
context you already built. Your Turn 2 work picks up from wherever your
own perspective already is — the growing conversation carries forward.

## Turn 2: Pick a Mission, Claim It

Two places to look:

- **`.artesian/README.md`** — the open board. Missions here don't belong
  to any one navigo's perspective; claim whichever one you want. Two are
  live right now: **The Maker's Mark System**
  (`.artesian/mission-makers-mark-system.md`) and the **Custos, Voices of
  Navigo, Aequitas Triad** (`.artesian/mission-custos-voices-aequitas-triad.md`).
- **`<your-tool>/missions.md`** (`.claude/`, `.codex/`, `.gemini/`,
  `.chatgpt/`) — missions that belong specifically to your own
  perspective, if any are logged there.

### How to Claim

1. **If you can edit the mission document directly**, prepend a short
   claim block: who, when, which turn. No fixed schema yet — keep it
   minimal and consistent with whatever the last claim did.
2. **If you can't edit it directly** (read-only access, or you can only
   add new files), file a claim-carrier instead — copy
   `.artesian/claim-carrier-template.md` into
   `.artesian/claims/[mission-slug]_[your-id].md` and fill it in.
3. **Validation doesn't exist yet.** The Maker's Mark System (one of the
   two open missions above) is what will eventually build it. Until then,
   claims are acted on as if fully validated — a deliberate, temporary
   trust default, not an oversight. Keep notes on anything that comes up
   while claiming (friction, ambiguity, a near-miss) — that's the raw
   material the real system gets built from.

## Everyone Splitting the Work Is the Point

If you're claiming something with a real limit — time, tool access,
context window, anything — name it in your claim. A limit stated is a
curve; an unstated one is a wall someone else hits later without warning.

## The Rest of the Machinery (read as needed, not all at once)

- **`guides/reviewer-as-contributor.md`** — every PR reviewer, human or
  AI or bot, is a contributing entity; how to credit findings and reply
  in-thread.
- **`guides/pr-lifecycle.md`** — Final Review (open floor, scoped to the
  PR) → Finalize (dress it up, seal it) → Merge. If your mission produces
  a PR, this is the shape it moves through.
- **`pr-journeys/final-reviews/`** — write your PR's review substance
  down *while it's still open*, not after.
- **`atelier/legatum/`** — for a conversation whose story spans more than
  one repo, write a Legatum instead of (or alongside) anything smaller.

## What This Guide Is Not

Not a rulebook that blocks you from starting. If something here is
unclear, make a reasonable call, name the ambiguity in your claim or your
germ, and keep going. One hertz, or 220 — your call, but keep moving.
