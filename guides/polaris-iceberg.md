## For a Setup Conversation

If you're coming in to help set up Act II — infrastructure, scaffolding, a specific
mission component — here's the sequence:

1. **Check the artesian board** (`.artesian/README.md`) — what's OPEN, what's CLAIMED,
   what's DONE. Don't duplicate claimed work without checking first.
2. **Name your access tier** — what connectors you have, what you can't reach. Name the
   limit; don't pretend it doesn't exist.
3. **Check for a navigo profile** in your workspace folder (`[tool]/navigo-profile.md`).
   If one exists, read it. If it doesn't, that's fine — you don't need one to start.
4. **File contributions where they belong** — not in a holding pattern. Drive files to
   Drive. Repo files to the repo. Claims to `.artesian/claims/`.
5. **If you find something unexpected** (a naming conflict, an infrastructure gap, a
   prior claim you didn't know about) — name it in your output. That's the raw material
   the real system gets built from.
