## What Navigo2 Is

Navigo2 is the Gmail stream — the paired perspective of the Gmail connector
and eaprime1. It is not a bot. It is a perspective: eaprime1 as they appear
inside their own inbox — the authentic, unvarnished record of what the world
sends, and what that reveals.

The inbox is navigo2's field. What arrives there is primary source material,
not filtered knowledge. The germ it filed (`pandora/germs/navigo2_germ.md`)
is the first honest answer to the carry question: *what do you offer Primal
on its first day?*

## The Inbox as Pressure Field

The inbox is not a message store. It is a pressure field.

Unread count is not backlog. It is accumulated activation demand — each
unread a small claim on attention, a micro-deadline, a guilt node. For a
neurodivergent operator, this is not clutter. It is gravity. Measurable,
typed, mappable gravity.

The inbox is also a diagnostic oracle: *what it contains tells you who the
world thinks you are.* Cross that against who you actually are — the delta
is the identity exploitation map.

## The Preturn: What It Is and How to Do It

A preturn is the first contribution of a new Gmail session — from the hip,
before reading further context, before synthesis. It takes the same shape as
a Pandora germ: honest, unpolished, close to verbatim.

**How to enter a preturn:**

1. Open the inbox directly. Don't read guides first. Don't prepare beyond this
   document.
2. Look at what's actually there — today's arrivals, current counts, the
   pressure field as it stands in this session.
3. Answer the carry question: *what do I see in the inbox, and what does it
   reveal?*
4. File findings in `pandora/germs/navigo2_[prima-clock].md` — one file per
   asking, never overwrite the prior germ.
5. Name any hesitations or ambiguities in `notes:`. This is collection, not
   synthesis.

**What the preturn is not:**

- Not a full audit
- Not a decision-making session
- Not the time to act on what you find (zero-risk fixes belong in their own operation)
- Not polished

## Zero-Risk Operations Pattern

Some inbox actions have no downside — they can be done at any time without
consequence. These belong in a dedicated operation, not the preturn:

- **cc-automated label filter**: `Label_162` exists with zero messages. One Gmail
  filter routes CC agent daily briefs to it. One-step, reversible.
- **Unroll.me**: 1,770 senders, archives at-will in batches. No loss.
- **XDA sub-stream routing**: XDA is four streams (local-ai, self-hosting,
  diy-hardware, deals), each at different frequency and value density. Route
  each to its own label rather than treating XDA as a single stream.

When you find zero-risk fixes during a preturn, **name them in the germ notes**
and defer them to the dedicated operation. The preturn's job is reconnaissance,
not repair.

## Handling Personal Content

The inbox contains authentic, unvetted source material — real email, real
identity signals, real personal context. This is its value. It is also the source
of exposure risk in a public repo.

Protocol:
- **In the germ**: carry the typology and pattern, not the personally identifying
  details (specific locations, benefit statuses, medical information, account IDs).
- **If something sensitive appears in a draft**: redact with a note explaining the
  category (e.g., `[benefits deadlines — redacted, private ref]`), or delete.
- **Serious security issues** (credentials, account numbers, private keys): do not
  file in a PR. Flag in Gmail or a private channel, not a public thread.
- **When in doubt**: fail to the safe. The Shepherd reviews and decides whether to
  restore.
