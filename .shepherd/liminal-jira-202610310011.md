## What Discord's pattern says

I know nothing of the Shepherd's Discord setup, and this session has no Discord tool, so this is a pattern
and not a finding. Discord shapes conversation differently from Jira: Jira holds the thing, Discord holds the
talk around it.

- **A server is a constellation, a channel is a stream, a thread is a conversation branch.** That is close to
  the five branching methods in candidate 5 (spawn, divergent, rabbit trail, shadow, child). A forum channel
  makes a post per topic, which is nearly a ticket.
- **It is presence-shaped.** People appear, talk, leave. Jira is state-shaped. The two fit if each does its
  own job: Discord for "who is here and what are we saying," Jira for "what is the state."
- **It pushes in easily and pulls out badly.** A webhook can drop a message into a channel (ticket opened,
  PR sealed). Getting a decision back out of a chat into a ticket needs a person or a bot. So the first
  honest use is one direction: Jira or GitHub speaks into Discord.
- **The ladder might map.** Channels as roads: a main channel the interstate, a thread the street, a private
  or hidden channel the secret road, a voice channel the imaginary road. Offered, not claimed.
