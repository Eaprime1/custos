My reading of that, marked as a reading: in blackjack you hit (draw one more) or stand (hold what you have).
Twenty-one is the mark, and over it is a bust. A soft hand can still change its ace; a hard hand cannot. That
fits the road we already have: `probatio` is the soft seal, `ultima probatio` the hard one, and `sustine` is
standing. "Perpetual" would be a table that never closes: you can leave a hand and return to it. The
Blackjack Iteration is the 21st, and 21 in the prime progression germ is where three 7s converge and the link to
math sits. I do not know whether that is the intended line. It is a line I noticed.

## What Discord's pattern says

*Corrected by the Shepherd (review comment, 202610310139 UTC): Discord is not a server of his. It is a good
idea, and it starts from a harmony server format. Every server must give him a way to interact, a simple UI
at minimum. A Discord server would be one built from the harmony format, and that is a large build. He thinks

*Handed on (202610310315 UTC): the working copy for harmony and Discord goes to the antigravity navigo as a semina in
`.antigravity/harmony-discord-semina.md`.*

I know nothing of the harmony format or of that server, and this session has no Discord tool, so what
follows is a pattern and not a finding. Discord shapes conversation differently from Jira: Jira holds the
thing, Discord holds the talk around it.

- **A server is a constellation, a channel is a stream, a thread is a conversation branch.** That is close to
  the five branching methods in candidate 5 (spawn, divergent, rabbit trail, shadow, child).
- **It is presence-shaped.** People appear, talk, leave. Jira is state-shaped. The two fit if each does its
  own job.
- **It pushes in easily and pulls out badly.** A webhook can drop a message into a channel; getting a decision
  back out needs a person or a bot. So the first honest use is one direction.

## How the work changes in a liminal

Turn by turn, I do one thing and stop. In a liminal there can be several strands at once: one gathering, one
building, one holding. The rules that do not move:

- Propose, then wait for a clear yes before changing statuses, labels on many issues, workflows or custody.
- Say every Jira change out loud and leave a ticket comment.
- I do not merge, and I describe the final seal in words.
- Each strand carries a stamp, so anything can be found again later.
