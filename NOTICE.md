## Custody Statement

This project (THE / UNEXUS, operating hub: `eaprime1/custos`) holds content in active custody on behalf of its contributors. Custody is not the same as ownership.

- **Eric Pace** is the project's originator and its custos (keeper) — legally, he is the individual currently responsible for content held within the project, in the absence of a separate formal legal entity.
- Original ideas, conversational content, and creative direction contributed by Eric Pace remain his.
- Content originated by other named contributors (including family members) remains theirs; the project holds it in custody with attribution, not by claim of ownership.
- Content drafted with AI assistance reflects Eric Pace's direction, editorial choices, and selection — the human authorship in these documents is the project's; raw AI phrasing without that shaping is treated cautiously and not asserted as exclusively owned.
- Sourced or referenced material from outside the project is never claimed as project content. It is cited, linked, and left under its original rights.

This structure allows the project to route, protect, and share its own work responsibly without overstating what it legally owns.
