# Mission: Build/Develop the Custos, Voices of Navigo, Aequitas Triad

**Status:** OPEN
**Prima-clock (named):** 202608311600
**Origin:** Navigo8's "A Note on Perspective" (`.custos/navigo8-perspective-shift-triad-note.md`), landed via the Breathe I import; held for Breathe II per eaprime1's direction

## The Note This Mission Builds From

Navigo8 named a shift, not a full system: three seats, confirmed but not
built out.

- **Custos** — keeper, router. Holds custody of what arrives and what's built.
- **Voices of Navigo** — the project's expressive layer. The aspects that speak.
- **Aequitas** — the conscience seat. Not a majority vote between the other
  two — the standard both are weighed against. "Does this hold up as fair,
  as agency-respecting, as something that could survive scrutiny."

Read `.custos/navigo8-perspective-shift-triad-note.md` in full before
starting — this mission summary doesn't replace it.

## Scope

1. **Give Aequitas an actual home.** Custos has `.custos/`; Voices of
   Navigo has the whole expressive layer of the project (the atelier,
   the navigo dot-folders, the round-robin itself). Aequitas has neither
   yet — decide whether it needs a dot-folder, a document, a role
   description, or something else entirely.
2. **Define what "weighed against Aequitas" actually means in practice.**
   The note is clear on the *shape* (a standard, not a vote) but not on
   the *mechanism* — is this a checklist a navigo runs before finalizing?
   A role someone/something holds? A set of questions asked at Final
   Review? This mission's real work is making the abstract concrete
   without flattening it into a rigid rulebook.
3. **Reconcile, don't just add.** This triad wasn't built in isolation —
   custos already has real practices doing adjacent work: the Ethics
   Check on every PR body, `guides/reviewer-as-contributor.md`'s
   disposition discipline, the Deck Master review gate in
   `docs/stale-branch-closure.md`. Figure out whether Aequitas *is* the
   name for something already partially built, or something genuinely
   new sitting alongside it.
4. **Name the open question Navigo8 itself flagged**: the note says
   "nothing here is final." Whoever picks this mission up should expect
   to find real ambiguity, not a spec waiting to be typed up.

## Why This Belongs on `.artesian`, Not a Dot-Folder

This isn't one navigo's perspective to develop alone — it names a
structure meant to sit *above* individual navigo perspectives (Aequitas
weighs Custos against Voices of Navigo, both of which span every navigo).
Open to whoever picks it up first, same as the Maker's Mark System.
